## 🌟 Module 1: The Legend, History & Global Coffee Belt

### 1. The Birthplace: Ethiopia & The Legend of Kaldi (850 CE)
* In the ancient forested highlands of **Kaffa, Ethiopia**, goat herder **Kaldi** observed his herd frolicking with joyful vitality after nibbling the bright red berries of an unfamiliar shrub.
* Chewing the cherries himself, Kaldi discovered renewed clarity and brought the fruit to local Sufi holy men.
* From Ethiopia, coffee traveled across the Red Sea into **Yemen**, where Sufi monks developed boiling and roasting techniques to sustain late-night prayers at the ancient export fortress port of **Al-Makha (Mocha)**.

### 2. The Global Coffee Belt (全球咖啡带)
* Coffee trees only flourish in the tropical band between **Latitude 25° North (Tropic of Cancer)** and **25° South (Tropic of Capricorn)**.
* **The Altitude Rule**: High-elevation farms ($1,400\text{m} - 2,200\text{m}$) experience cool mountain nights. This slows cherry maturation, allowing trees more time to pack dense sugars, aromatic esters, and bright organic acids (malic, citric, phosphoric) into the seed.

## 🌿 Module 2: Bean Varietals & Botanical Anatomy

### 1. Arabica vs. Robusta
| Characteristic | *Coffea Arabica* (阿拉比卡) | *Coffea Canephora / Robusta* (罗布斯塔) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Market Share** | 60% – 70% (Specialty Standard) | 30% – 40% (Commercial / Instant / Crema) |
| **Chromosomes** | **44** (Tetraploid, self-pollinating) | **22** (Diploid) |
| **Optimal Altitude** | $1,000\text{m} - 2,200\text{m}$ (High mountain) | Sea level to $800\text{m}$ (Warm lowlands) |
| **Natural Sugars** | **6% – 9%** (Twice as sweet!) | 3% – 5% |
| **Lipids & Oils** | **15% – 17%** (Silky mouthfeel) | 10% – 11% |
| **Caffeine Level** | **1.1% – 1.5%** (Gentle & smooth) | 2.2% – 2.7% (Natural pest defense) |
| **Cup Flavour** | Delicate jasmine, peach, citrus, chocolate | Earthy, woody, bitter dark cacao, rubbery |

### 2. Famous Specialty Arabica Lineages
1. **Panama Geisha / Gesha (瑰夏)**: Originating in Gesha, Ethiopia; world-famous for intoxicating aromas of jasmine, bergamot, orange blossom, and white peach.
2. **Typica (铁皮卡)**: The ancient foundational cultivar; clean cup with sweet apple acidity and tea clarity.
3. **Bourbon & Yellow Bourbon (波旁 / 黄波旁)**: High natural sugar content yielding rich caramel, roasted almond, and milk chocolate notes.
4. **Kenya SL-28 & SL-34**: Bred in Kenya's Scott Labs; famous for explosive blackcurrant, ruby grapefruit, and juicy tomato sweetness.

## ☀️ Module 3: Green Bean Processing & Roasting Chemistry

### 1. Processing Methods
* **Washed / Wet (水洗法)**: Skin & pulp removed immediately; soaked in water fermentation tanks for 12-36 hours. Produces the cleanest cup, sparkling citrus acidity, and tea-like delicacy.
* **Natural / Dry (日晒法)**: Whole cherries dried in the sun on raised beds for 20-30 days with sweet pulp intact. Infuses wild strawberry, blueberry jam, tropical fruit, and winey body.
* **Honey (蜜处理法)**: Skin is peeled, but the sticky mucilage layer is left on the bean to dry slowly under shade/sun. Balances crisp acidity with honey and apricot sweetness.

### 2. Roasting Spectrum
* **Light (City / Cinnamon)**: $195^\circ\text{C} - 205^\circ\text{C}$ (Just past First Crack). Maximum floral & fruit notes, sparkling acidity, zero bitterness.
* **Medium (City+)**: $210^\circ\text{C} - 218^\circ\text{C}$ (Caramelization peak). Superb balance of soft acidity, ripe fruit, and sweet milk chocolate.
* **Medium-Dark (Full City)**: $220^\circ\text{C} - 228^\circ\text{C}$ (Second crack whispers). Surface oils appear. Rich dark chocolate and toasted nuts.
* **Dark (French / Italian)**: $230^\circ\text{C}+$. Bold smoky cacao, heavy crema, low acidity. Ideal for milky lattes.

## ☕ Module 4: Mama's Golden Hand-Pour Brewing Compass

### Golden Pour-Over (V60) Recipe
* **Dose**: 15 g fresh medium-fine grounds
* **Water**: 240 g clean filtered water ($1:16$ ratio)
* **Water Temp**: $91^\circ\text{C} - 93^\circ\text{C}$ ($196^\circ\text{F} - 200^\circ\text{F}$)
* **Total Time**: 2m 15s – 2m 30s
* **Steps**:
  1. **Bloom (0:00 - 0:35)**: Pour 45 g water evenly to release $\text{CO}_2$ gas bubbles.
  2. **1st Pour (0:35 - 1:15)**: Spiral pour up to 140 g water in smooth concentric circles.
  3. **2nd Pour (1:15 - 1:50)**: Spiral pour up to 240 g water.
  4. **Drawdown (1:50 - 2:20)**: Let water draw down through flat coffee bed. Enjoy!
