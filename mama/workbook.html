2. Green Bean Processing & Roasting Spectrum (处理法与烘焙度)

💧 Washed (水洗法): Pulp removed → Fermented in water tanks (12-36h) → Washed clean with mountain water → Dried. Result: Sparkling citric clarity, transparent florals, tea-like finish.
☀️ Natural (日晒法): Whole cherry dried in the sun for 25-30 days with sweet pulp intact. Result: Intense strawberry, blueberry jam, winey body, heavy sweetness.
🍯 Honey (蜜处理): Pulp peeled off, but sticky sugar mucilage remains during sun-drying. Result: Harmonious honey and apricot sweetness with soft acidity.
