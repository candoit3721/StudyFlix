# 🧪 Matter, Mixtures & Chemical Reactions! 🔬
*(Grade 5 Core Science & Kitchen Lab Secrets)*

Matter is anything that has **mass** (weight) and takes up **space** (volume). Everything around you is made of matter!

---

## 🧊 1. The States of Matter & Particle Party

Imagine atoms and molecules like kids on a dance floor:

| State | How the Particles Behave | Shape & Volume | Real-Life Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solid** 🧊 | Tightly packed together, vibrating in place. | Fixed shape, fixed volume. | Ice cube, wooden desk, gold bar |
| **Liquid** 💧 | Flowing around each other, sliding freely. | Takes shape of container, fixed volume. | Water, juice, olive oil |
| **Gas** 💨 | Zooming around super fast with tons of empty space! | Expands to fill any container and volume. | Steam, oxygen, balloon helium |

---

## 💥 2. Physical Change vs. Chemical Change: The Big Detective Mystery!

* **Physical Change**: The substance changes its look, shape, or phase, but **NO new chemical is made**. (Often reversible!)
  * *Examples*: Melting ice, ripping paper, chopping vegetables, dissolving sugar in warm water.
* **Chemical Change**: A chemical reaction happens, bonds break & reform, and a **BRAND NEW substance is created!** (Usually cannot be undone!)
  * *4 Clues of a Chemical Change*:
    1. 🫧 **Bubbles / Gas created** (without boiling)
    2. 🔥 **Temperature change** (gets suddenly hot or cold)
    3. 🎨 **Unexpected color change** (like iron rusting into reddish flakes)
    4. 🍞 **New odor or smell** (like toast browning or milk spoiling)

---

## 🥤 3. Mixtures vs. Solutions: What's in Your Drink?

* **Mixture**: Two or more things mixed together where you can easily see or separate the parts!
  * *Example*: Trail mix (peanuts, raisins, M&Ms), fruit salad, sand in water.
* **Solution**: A special kind of mixture where one substance **dissolves completely** and spreads evenly so it looks clear and uniform!
  * **Solute**: The solid that gets dissolved (e.g., Sugar or Salt).
  * **Solvent**: The liquid that does the dissolving (e.g., Water — known as the *Universal Solvent*!).
  * *How do you get the salt back?* Evaporate the water!

---

## ⚖️ 4. The Law of Conservation of Matter (The Magic Scale Rule)
> **Rule**: Matter cannot be created out of nowhere, and it cannot be destroyed! Atoms just get rearranged!
> 
> *If you mix 10 grams of baking soda with 50 grams of vinegar in a closed bottle, the final bubbly mixture will weigh **EXACTLY 60 grams**!*
