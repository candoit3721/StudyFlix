# 🌲 Ecosystems, Food Webs & The Circle of Life! 🦅
*(Grade 5 Ecology & Energy Flow Adventure)*

Every living organism on planet Earth is connected in a giant web of energy! From the tiniest blade of grass to the soaring bald eagle, let's explore how life thrives.

---

## ☀️ 1. The Super Roles in Every Ecosystem

```
     ☀️ THE SUN (Original Energy Source)
            │
            ▼
    🌱 PRODUCERS (Make their own food via Photosynthesis)
            │
            ▼
    🐰 PRIMARY CONSUMERS (Herbivores that eat plants)
            │
            ▼
    🦊 SECONDARY CONSUMERS (Carnivores / Omnivores)
            │
            ▼
    🦅 APEX PREDATORS (Top of the food chain, no natural enemies)
            │
            ▼
    🍄 DECOMPOSERS (Recycle dead plants/animals back into rich soil!)
```

### Key Vocabulary:
* **Producers (Plants, Algae)**: Use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) to make sugar (glucose) and oxygen ($O_2$) through **Photosynthesis**.
* **Herbivores**: Plant-only eaters (Rabbits, Deer, Caterpillars).
* **Carnivores**: Meat-only eaters (Wolves, Hawks, Sharks).
* **Omnivores**: Eat BOTH plants and meat (Bears, Raccoons, Humans!).
* **Decomposers (Fungi, Worms, Bacteria)**: Nature’s ultimate clean-up crew and recyclers!

---

## ⚡ 2. The 10% Energy Pyramid Rule!

Did you know? When an animal eats another organism, **only about 10% of the energy** gets passed up to the next level!
The rest (90%) is used up by the organism to run, breathe, stay warm, and live.

* 🌱 **1,000 Calories** in Grass
* 🦗 **100 Calories** stored in Grasshoppers
* 🐸 **10 Calories** stored in Frogs
* 🦅 **1 Calorie** stored in the Hawk!
