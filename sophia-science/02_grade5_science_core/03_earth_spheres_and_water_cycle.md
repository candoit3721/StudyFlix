# 🌎 Earth's 4 Spheres & The Water Cycle Journey! 💧
*(Grade 5 Earth & Space Systems)*

Planet Earth is an incredible giant spaceship where 4 major systems (spheres) work together 24/7 to keep life alive and thriving!

---

## 🌐 1. The Four Spheres of Earth

```
       ┌───────────────────────────────┐
       │   🌬️ ATMOSPHERE (The Air)     │
       └──────────────┬────────────────┘
                      │
   ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
   ▼                                     ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────────┐
│  💧 HYDROSPHERE (Water) │   │ 🪨 GEOSPHERE (Rocks/Land)│
└────────────┬────────────┘   └────────────┬─────────────┘
             │                             │
             └──────────────┬──────────────┘
                            ▼
               ┌────────────────────────┐
               │ 🦁 BIOSPHERE (Living)  │
               └────────────────────────┘
```

1. **🌬️ Atmosphere**: The layer of gases surrounding Earth (78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, carbon dioxide, clouds). Protects us from meteors and solar radiation!
2. **💧 Hydrosphere**: All the water on Earth — oceans, lakes, rivers, glaciers, ice caps, underground aquifers, and rain. (Covers ~71% of Earth's surface!).
3. **🪨 Geosphere**: All rocks, minerals, mountains, sand dunes, tectonic plates, and magma inside Earth's core.
4. **🦁 Biosphere**: All living organisms — humans, animals, plants, fungi, and microscopic bacteria.

> **Sphere Interactions**: When a volcano (**Geosphere**) erupts ash into the air (**Atmosphere**), blocks sunlight from trees (**Biosphere**), and causes acid rain into rivers (**Hydrosphere**), all 4 spheres interact!

## 💧 2. The Amazing Water Cycle (Follow "Drippy" the Water Drop!)

```
          ☀️ SUN (Heats up surface water)
               │
       ┌───────┴────────┐
       ▼                ▼
 🌊 EVAPORATION   🌿 TRANSPIRATION (Plants release water vapor)
       │                │
       └───────┬────────┘
               ▼
       ☁️ CONDENSATION (Vapor cools to form clouds)
               │
               ▼
       🌧️ PRECIPITATION (Rain, Snow, Sleet, Hail)
               │
               ▼
       🏔️ RUNOFF & GROUNDWATER (Flows back to rivers & oceans)
```

* **Evaporation**: Liquid water heats up and turns into invisible water vapor gas.
* **Transpiration**: Plants "sweat" water vapor out of tiny pores on their leaves (called stomata).
* **Condensation**: Cold air high up makes water vapor turn back into tiny liquid droplets, forming clouds!
* **Precipitation**: When cloud droplets get too heavy, they fall as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
