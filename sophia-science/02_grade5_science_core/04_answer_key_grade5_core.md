## 🧪 File 01 Answers: Matter, Mixtures & Reactions

### Part 1: Physical (P) or Chemical (C) Change
1. Melting ice cream: **P (Physical)** — Still ice cream, just changed from solid to liquid!
2. Frying an egg: **C (Chemical)** — Heat permanently changes proteins in egg white/yolk.
3. Chopping an apple: **P (Physical)** — Only shape and size changed.
4. Baking a chocolate cake: **C (Chemical)** — Heat reacts baking powder, eggs, sugar into fluffy sponge cake with new gas pockets.
5. Silver spoon tarnishing: **C (Chemical)** — Silver reacts with sulfur in the air to form silver sulfide ($Ag_2S$).
6. Crushing a soda can: **P (Physical)** — Still aluminum metal, just deformed.
7. Vinegar + baking soda volcano: **C (Chemical)** — Creates carbon dioxide gas bubbles ($CO_2$), water, and sodium acetate!

### Part 2: The Mystery Lemonade Stand
1. **Dissolved into a solution** (the sugar molecules spread out between water molecules).
2. **Solute**: Sugar (and lemon juice concentrates) | **Solvent**: Water.
3. **Total weight**: $20\text{g} + 200\text{g} + 30\text{g} = $ **250 grams** *(Conservation of Mass!)*.
4. **Left behind**: Sugar crystals and lemon solids (water evaporates into the air as gas, leaving the dissolved solid solute behind).

### Part 3: Kitchen Science Riddle
* **Gas** (highest particle energy, expands to fill any volume).

## 🌲 File 02 Answers: Ecosystems & Energy Flow

### Challenge 1: Food Web Detective
1. **Producer**: Oak Tree (leaves/acorns).
2. **Primary Consumer**: Squirrel (eats acorns) or Caterpillar (eats leaves).
3. **Secondary Consumer**: Robin (eats caterpillars) or Red Fox (eats squirrels).
4. **Decomposer**: Mushroom (fungi).
5. **4-step Food Chain**:  
   `[ Sun ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Oak Leaves ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Caterpillar ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Robin ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Hawk ]`  
   *(or `[ Sun ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Acorns ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Squirrel ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Fox ]` $\rightarrow$ `[ Hawk ]`)*

### Challenge 2: "What Happens If...?"
* **Scenario A (Bees)**: Without bees to pollinate flowers, plants cannot fertilize to make seeds and fruits. Fruit production will plummet, leading to food shortages for birds and mammals!
* **Scenario B (Yellowstone Wolves)**: **B) Apex predators help keep the entire ecosystem in healthy balance.** (This is called a *Trophic Cascade*!).

### Challenge 3: Who Eats What?
1. Earthworm: **Decomposer**
2. Koala: **Herbivore**
3. Grizzly bear: **Omnivore**
4. Great White Shark: **Carnivore**

## 🌎 File 03 Answers: Earth's Spheres & Water Cycle

### Challenge 1: Which Sphere Is It?
1. Glacier: **Hydrosphere** (specifically the cryosphere, frozen water)
2. Mount Everest summit: **Geosphere**
3. Whales singing: **Biosphere**
4. Wind pushing sailboat: **Atmosphere**
5. Desert sand dunes: **Geosphere**
6. Dew drops: **Hydrosphere**

### Challenge 2: Sphere Interaction Detective
1. Tree roots (**Biosphere**) break rocks (**Geosphere**).
2. Ocean hurricane (**Hydrosphere**) impacts air/winds (**Atmosphere**).
3. Cow (**Biosphere**) drinks water (**Hydrosphere**).

### Challenge 3: Water Cycle Time Machine
* *Scientific Explanation*: Earth is a closed system for matter. Water molecules ($H_2O$) on Earth today are the very same water molecules that existed millions of years ago! They continuously cycle through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, transpiration, and groundwater flow without ever leaving our planet.
