## 🧊 Unit 1: Matter, Changes & The Kitchen Chemistry Lab

### 📖 The Science Story: The Magic Pancake Breakfast
On Saturday morning, Sophia helps make blueberry pancakes:
1. She measures flour, sugar, baking powder, milk, and eggs into a bowl (**Mixture**).
2. She stirs until the sugar crystals dissolve completely (**Solution**).
3. She pours the batter onto a hot sizzling skillet. Bubbles of carbon dioxide gas form and pop, the batter fluffs up, and the sweet golden-brown pancake aroma fills the kitchen (**Chemical Change**).
4. She melts a pat of butter over the warm pancake stack (**Physical Change**).

```
               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │              PHYSICAL vs. CHEMICAL CHANGE              │
               │                                                        │
               │  🧊 PHYSICAL CHANGE:                                   │
               │  • Only changes shape, size, or state of matter.       │
               │  • No new molecules created. (Reversible!)             │
               │  • Examples: Melting ice, dissolving sugar, tearing.   │
               │                                                        │
               │  🔥 CHEMICAL CHANGE:                                   │
               │  • Chemical bonds break & form NEW substances.         │
               │  • 4 Clues: Gas bubbles, color change, temp change,    │
               │    new odor or light emitted!                          │
               │  • Examples: Baking a cake, rusting iron, fireworks.   │
               └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 🌲 Unit 2: Ecosystems, Food Webs & The Circle of Life

### 📖 The Science Story: The Sun's Energy in Your Apple
Every single movement you make — riding your bike, solving math problems, dancing — is powered by energy that originally came from the **Sun** 93 million miles away!

1. ☀️ **The Sun** beams light energy to Earth.
2. 🌱 **Producers (Green Plants)** absorb sunlight, water ($H_2O$), and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) through their leaves. Using **Photosynthesis**, they transform this into sweet glucose sugar ($C_6H_{12}O_6$) and release oxygen ($O_2$)!
3. 🐰 **Primary Consumers (Herbivores)** eat the plants to get energy.
4. 🦊 **Secondary Consumers (Carnivores/Omnivores)** eat the herbivores.
5. 🍄 **Decomposers (Mushrooms, Earthworms, Bacteria)** break down fallen leaves and dead organisms, recycling valuable nutrients back into the soil so new plants can grow!

```
    ☀️ SUN  ──>  🌱 GRASS (Producer)  ──>  🦗 GRASSHOPPER (Herbivore)
                                                      │
                                                      ▼
    🍄 MUSHROOM (Decomposer) <── 🦅 HAWK <── 🐸 FROG (Carnivore)
```

## 🌎 Unit 3: Earth's 4 Spheres & The Water Cycle Time Machine

### 📖 The Science Story: The Dinosaur Water Drop
Water on Earth is ancient! Because Earth is a closed system for matter, the exact same water molecules have been recycling for over **4 billion years**. A drop of water in your morning glass might have evaporated from a prehistoric ocean, rained onto a Stegosaurus, frozen into an Ice Age glacier, and flowed into a river today!

```
     🌬️ ATMOSPHERE (Air Blanket)        💧 HYDROSPHERE (Oceans, Rivers, Ice)
                  \                                /
                   \      🌍 THE 4 SPHERES        /
                    \                            /
     🪨 GEOSPHERE (Rocks, Mountains)     🦁 BIOSPHERE (All Living Things)
```

* **Evaporation**: Liquid water heats up into invisible water vapor.
* **Transpiration**: Plants "sweat" water vapor out of leaf pores.
* **Condensation**: Cold air cools water vapor into clouds.
* **Precipitation**: Clouds release rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
* **Runoff / Infiltration**: Water flows into rivers, lakes, and underground aquifers.
