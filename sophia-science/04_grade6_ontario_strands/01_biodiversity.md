## 📖 Part 1: Real-World Context & Concept Spotlight

In 1988 a cargo ship sailed into Lake St. Clair and emptied its ballast water.
Hidden in that water were zebra mussels, a species from eastern Europe that had never lived in North America.
Within ten years they had spread through all five Great Lakes.
A single zebra mussel filters about 1 L of water every day, stripping out the microscopic phytoplankton that everything else depends on.
The water got clearer, and people thought that was good news.
It was not.
Clearer water meant the food at the base of the web was gone.

### 💡 Concept 1: Biodiversity Has Three Levels

```
    WITHIN a species        BETWEEN species          Of ECOSYSTEMS
    (genetic variety)       (how many species)       (variety of habitats)
    ┌───────────────┐       ┌───────────────┐        ┌───────────────┐
    │ grey wolf     │       │ wolf, moose,  │        │ boreal forest,│
    │ black wolf    │       │ beaver, loon, │        │ wetland,      │
    │ white wolf    │       │ trillium ...  │        │ Great Lakes   │
    └───────────────┘       └───────────────┘        └───────────────┘
         narrowest    ────────────────────────────>      widest
```

### 💡 Concept 2: The Six Kingdoms

Classification is based on **observable characteristics**, never on where something lives.
Ask three questions in order: how many cells, is there a nucleus, and how does it get its food?

| Kingdom | Cells | Nucleus | Cell wall | Gets food by | Canadian example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Animalia** | Many | Yes | No | Eating | Moose, monarch butterfly |
| **Plantae** | Many | Yes | Yes (cellulose) | Photosynthesis | Sugar maple, white trillium |
| **Fungi** | Many or one | Yes | Yes (chitin) | Absorbing | Morel mushroom, bread mould |
| **Protista** | Usually one | Yes | Sometimes | Both ways | Amoeba, paramecium |
| **Eubacteria** | One | **No** | Yes | Both ways | E. coli, Lake Erie cyanobacteria |
| **Archaebacteria** | One | **No** | Yes | Both ways | Hot spring thermophile |

**Memory hack:** the last two are the only kingdoms with **no nucleus**.
Archaebacteria are the "ancient extremists" that live in boiling, salty or acidic places.

### 💡 Concept 3: Vertebrates and Invertebrates

A **vertebrate** has a backbone. There are exactly five classes.

```
  FISH        AMPHIBIAN      REPTILE        BIRD          MAMMAL
  gills       moist skin     dry scales     feathers      fur + milk
  lake trout  salamander     Blanding's     common loon   beaver
                             turtle
```

An **invertebrate** has no backbone, and that is about 97% of all animal species.
Molluscs (zebra mussel), insects (monarch), arachnids (wolf spider), crustaceans, worms.

### 💡 Concept 4: The 10% Energy Rule

Only about **10%** of the energy at one level passes to the next.
The other 90% is used for living or lost as heat.

```
  Phytoplankton ──10%──> Zooplankton ──10%──> Yellow perch ──10%──> Lake trout
     50 000 kJ            5 000 kJ             500 kJ               50 kJ
```

This is why top predators are always rare, and why removing the base of a web is catastrophic.

### 💡 Concept 5: Symbiosis, the Three Relationships

| Relationship | Species 1 | Species 2 | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mutualism** | Benefits | Benefits | Bee and flower |
| **Commensalism** | Benefits | Unaffected | Barnacle on a whale |
| **Parasitism** | Benefits | **Harmed** | Sea lamprey on lake trout |
