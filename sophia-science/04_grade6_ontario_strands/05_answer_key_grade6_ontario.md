## 📄 00: Master Sheet, Cross-Strand Warm-Up

**1.** Life Systems (Biodiversity), Matter and Energy (Electricity), Structures and Mechanisms (Flight), Earth and Space Systems (Space).

**2.** Mass: kilogram (kg). Weight: newton (N). Air pressure: kilopascal (kPa). Electrical energy on a bill: kilowatt hour (kWh). Voltage: volt (V). Speed: kilometres per hour (km/h).

**3.** **weight**, **lift**, **drag**.
*Why:* mass, kilogram and distance are not forces. Forces are measured in newtons.

**4.** Independent: wing camber (the curve). Dependent: lift produced. Controlled: fan speed, wing area, wing material, air temperature (any two).

**5.** An invasive species has no natural predators in its new home, so it multiplies and outcompetes or destroys native species, which lowers the number of species present.

**6.** In parallel each device has its own path back to the source, so a break in one branch leaves every other branch complete and the rest of the house stays lit.

**7.** Earth's axis is tilted 23.5 degrees and always points the same way, so each hemisphere leans toward the Sun for half the year and receives more direct sunlight and longer days.

**8.** Lift, produced because air moves faster over the curved upper surface of the wing, lowering the pressure there so the higher pressure underneath pushes the wing up.

## 🧬 01: Biodiversity

### ⭐ Tier 1

**1.** Morel mushroom: **Fungi**. Sugar maple: **Plantae**. Amoeba: **Protista**. Moose: **Animalia**. Hot spring thermophile: **Archaebacteria**. E. coli: **Eubacteria**.

**2.** **Eubacteria** and **Archaebacteria**.
*Why:* these are the only two kingdoms whose cells have no nucleus.

**3.** Invertebrates: **zebra mussel, monarch butterfly, wolf spider**.
*Why:* lake trout is a fish, common loon a bird, beaver a mammal, and all three have backbones.

**4.** Fish (lake trout), Amphibian (blue-spotted salamander), Reptile (Blanding's turtle), Bird (common loon), Mammal (beaver or moose).

**5.** Biodiversity is the variety of life on Earth, measured at three levels: genetic variety **within** a species, the number of different **species** in a place, and the variety of **ecosystems**.

### ⭐⭐ Tier 2

**6.** Zooplankton: 80 000 × 10% = **8 000 kJ**. Yellow perch: 8 000 × 10% = **800 kJ**. Lake trout: 800 × 10% = **80 kJ**.
*Why:* the 10% rule applies once at every transfer, so three transfers means multiplying by 0.1 three times.

**7.** Lamprey on trout: **parasitism** (one gains, one is harmed). Bee and flower: **mutualism** (both gain). Bird nesting in an unaffected tree: **commensalism** (one gains, one unaffected).

**8.** Zebra mussels filter out the phytoplankton, so the **producers** crash first. Zooplankton eat phytoplankton, so they fall next. Perch eat zooplankton, so they fall after that. Lake trout eat perch, so they fall last. The damage travels **upward** from the base, which makes this a **bottom-up collapse**.

**9.** The lake trout is the top predator that normally eats yellow perch. With the trout gone, far fewer perch are eaten each year, so the perch population is **released from predation** and grows. Because the change starts at the top and works downward, this is a **trophic cascade** (top-down).

**10.** Any two, with reasons:
• **Ocean ballast water exchange rules**, because ballast water was the arrival route for the zebra mussel, so blocking it prevents new invasions.
• **Lampricide treatment of spawning streams**, because it targets the sea lamprey directly without introducing another new species.
• **Protecting wetlands and shorelines**, because habitat loss is a leading cause of species becoming endangered.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Tier 3

**11.** **32 species.** Each question doubles the number of possible paths: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32. After 5 questions there are $2^5 = 32$ end points.

**12.** **5 years.**
Year 1: 6 400 ÷ 2 = 3 200. Year 2: 1 600. Year 3: 800. Year 4: 400. Year 5: 200.
Year 4 gives exactly 400, which is not *below* 400, so the first year below 400 is year **5**.
*Teaching note:* the wording "below 400" is the trap. 400 is not below 400.

**13.** **Nothing certain.** The rule is: backbone → lungs. It does not say that *only* vertebrates have lungs. A snail has a lung and no backbone. Reversing an "all A are B" statement is a logic error (affirming the consequent).

**14.** • **B is affected first, and it falls.** B ate C, so B immediately loses its food source.
• **D is most likely to increase.** C was D's predator, so D is released from predation.
• A falls later, because A depends on B, which is one further step along the chain.

**15.** • Independent variable: **salt concentration**.
• Flaw: only the 0 g/L tank is in sunlight, so **light** changes between groups as well as salt.
• Why it ruins the conclusion: a fair test changes exactly **one** thing. With two variables differing, any difference in hatching could have been caused by the light instead of the salt, so no conclusion about salt is valid.

## ✈️ 02: Flight & Aerodynamics

### ⭐ Tier 1

**1.** Opposes weight: **lift**. Opposes thrust: **drag**. Acts forward: **thrust**. Acts downward: **weight**.

**2.** Air has **mass**; air takes up **space**; air can be **compressed**; air exerts **pressure**.

**3.** "Where air moves **faster**, its pressure is **lower**."

**4.** **lift = weight** (level flight) and **thrust = drag** (steady speed).
*Why:* "straight and level" balances the vertical pair; "steady speed" balances the horizontal pair.

**5.** Any one: hollow lightweight bones, which reduce **weight**. Overlapping feathers forming a smooth surface, which reduces **drag**. A deep keel bone anchoring flight muscles, which increases **thrust**.

### ⭐⭐ Tier 2

**6.** Vertical: 310 − 287 = **+23 kN**, so it is **climbing**.
Horizontal: 40 − 46 = **−6 kN**, so it is **slowing down**.

**7.**

| Lift | Weight | Thrust | Drag | Vertical | Horizontal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 290 | 290 | 55 | 44 | Level (balanced) | Speeding up |
| 260 | 290 | 44 | 44 | Descending | Steady speed |
| 330 | 290 | 40 | 52 | Climbing | Slowing down |

**8.** A **stall**. Past about 15 degrees the airflow **separates** from the curved upper surface into turbulence. Without smooth fast flow there is no low-**pressure** region above the wing, so the pressure difference and therefore the lift collapse.

**9.** It must **fly faster** or use a **larger angle of attack**. Lift comes from pushing air downward, and thin air means fewer air molecules meet the wing each second. Meeting the air faster, or deflecting it more steeply, restores the same lift. This is why airliners cruise near 850 km/h high up but land at about 250 km/h.

**10.** An aircraft burns nearly the same fuel whether it is half full or completely full, because the fuel is dominated by moving the aircraft itself. Emissions **per passenger** are total emissions divided by the number of passengers, so doubling the passengers on the same flight roughly halves each person's share.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Tier 3

**11.** **270 kN.** Speed ratio = 450 ÷ 300 = 1.5. Lift goes with speed **squared**, so the lift ratio is $1.5^2 = 2.25$. New lift = 120 × 2.25 = 270 kN.

**12.** **60 000 N.** Added mass = 6 000 L × 1 kg/L = 6 000 kg. Weight = $m \times g$ = 6 000 × 10 = 60 000 N (60 kN).

**13.** **5 kN more.** In level flight lift = weight = 240 kN, and thrust = drag.
Aircraft P: drag = 240 ÷ 16 = 15 kN. Aircraft Q: drag = 240 ÷ 12 = 20 kN.
Difference = 20 − 15 = **5 kN**.

**14.** **96 km.** A 40:1 glide ratio means forward distance = 40 × height lost = 2 400 × 40 = 96 000 m = **96 km**.

**15.** **34.4%.**
Old: 5 400 ÷ 72 = 75 kg per passenger.
New fuel burn: 5 400 × 0.82 = 4 428 kg. New: 4 428 ÷ 90 = 49.2 kg per passenger.
Drop = (75 − 49.2) ÷ 75 = 25.8 ÷ 75 = 0.344 = **34.4%**.
*Teaching note:* the common wrong answer is 18%, which ignores the extra passengers.

## 🚀 03: Space & the Solar System

### ⭐ Tier 1

**1.** **rotation**; **revolution**.

**2.** In space: **meteoroid**. In the atmosphere: **meteor** (shooting star). On the ground: **meteorite**.

**3.** **(b) Earth's 23.5 degree axial tilt.**
(a) is wrong because Earth is actually **closest** to the Sun in early January, during a Canadian winter.
(c) is wrong because the Sun's output is essentially constant through the year.

**4.** Natural: **Sun, comet, asteroid, planet, moon**. RADARSAT, the Canadarm and GPS satellites are built by people, so they are artificial.

**5.** The **Canadarm** (later **Canadarm2**, with the Dextre manipulator).

### ⭐⭐ Tier 2

**6.** Mass on the Moon: **40 kg** (mass never changes). Weight on the Moon: 40 × 1.6 = **64 N**. Weight on Earth: 40 × 9.81 = **392.4 N**.

**7.**

| World | $g$ | Mass | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | 9.81 | 25 kg | 245.25 N |
| Mars | 3.7 | 25 kg | 92.5 N |
| Jupiter | 24.8 | 25 kg | 620 N |

*Key teaching point:* the mass column never changes. Only the weight does.

**8.** Two reasons: (1) Neptune orbits at about 4 495 million km against Mercury's 58 million km, so its **path around the Sun is enormously longer**. (2) Gravity **weakens with distance**, so Neptune also travels more slowly along that longer path.

**9.** Earth's **axis** always points the same way in space. In December the northern **hemisphere** leans away from the Sun, giving Toronto short days and a low sun, which means at that same moment the southern **hemisphere** must be leaning toward the Sun, giving Sydney long days.

**10.** It proves the tail is **pushed outward by something coming from the Sun**, namely the solar wind and the pressure of sunlight. If the tail were simply material left behind by motion, it would trail *behind* the direction of travel, and on the outbound leg it does not.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Tier 3

**11.** **500 seconds**, which is **8 minutes 20 seconds**.
time = distance ÷ speed = 150 000 000 ÷ 300 000. Cancel five zeros from each: 1 500 ÷ 3 = 500 s. 500 ÷ 60 = 8 min 20 s.

**12.** **Mercury 5, Venus 1.**
Mercury: 440 ÷ 88 = 5 exactly, so 5 complete orbits.
Venus: 440 ÷ 225 = 1.96, and only whole orbits count, so 1 complete orbit.

**13.** **22 cm.** Size ratio = 142 984 ÷ 12 756 = 11.2, so Jupiter is about 11.2 Earths wide. Model width = 2 cm × 11.2 = 22.4 cm ≈ **22 cm**.
*Teaching note:* answering 11 cm is the classic slip, forgetting that Earth was drawn as 2 cm, not 1 cm.

**14.** **45 N under.** Weight on Mars = 150 × 3.7 = 555 N. Margin = 600 − 555 = **45 N**.
On Earth: 150 × 9.81 = 1 471.5 N, which is far **over** the 600 N limit.

**15.** **2 190 Planet X days.**
One Planet X year in Earth hours = 4 × 365 × 24 = 35 040 h.
Days per year = 35 040 ÷ 16 = **2 190**.

## ⚡ 04: Electricity & Electrical Devices

### ⭐ Tier 1

**1.** Type: **static electricity**. The spark: a **discharge**.

**2.** A **source** of energy (battery), **conductors** (wires), a **load** (bulb or device), and an **unbroken path** (a complete circuit).

**3.** Conductors (**C**): copper wire, salt water, toonie. Insulators (**I**): rubber glove, glass marble, dry maple stick.
*Note:* the word **dry** matters. Wet wood contains dissolved minerals and can conduct.

**4.** Kettle: **thermal (heat)**. LED bulb: **light**. Speaker: **sound**. Blender: **kinetic (motion)**.

**5.** **In parallel.** Each light has its own separate path back to the source, so breaking one branch leaves the other branches complete. It is done this way so that a single failed bulb cannot darken the whole house.

### ⭐⭐ Tier 2

**6.** **All four bulbs go out.** A series circuit is a single loop, so the current must pass through every component in turn. Unscrewing bulb 3 leaves a gap in the one and only loop, so no current can flow anywhere. This is exactly why old Christmas light strings all failed at once.

**7.** 6 ÷ 3 = **2 V** each. With a fourth bulb: 6 ÷ 4 = **1.5 V** each. The brightness **decreases**, because each bulb now receives less voltage.

**8.** Each bulb gets the full **9 V**. With bulb 1 unscrewed, **2** stay lit. In parallel each bulb has its own branch back to the battery, so breaking one branch leaves the other two branches complete.

**9.** **0.5 kWh.** Convert power: 1 500 W = 1.5 kW. Convert time: 20 min = 20 ÷ 60 = ⅓ h. Energy = 1.5 × ⅓ = **0.5 kWh** (about 6 cents in Ontario).

**10.** A current is charge **moving**, so a conductor needs charged particles free to move. Pure water molecules are neutral and very few come apart, so almost nothing is free to carry charge. Dissolving salt splits it into positive and negative **charged particles** that drift through the water and carry the current. Lake and tap water contain dissolved minerals, which is why they conduct and why water near electricity is dangerous.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Tier 3

**11.** **$111.69 saved.**
Power saved per bulb = 60 − 9 = 51 W. For ten bulbs = 510 W = 0.51 kW.
Energy saved per day = 0.51 × 5 = 2.55 kWh.
Energy saved per year = 2.55 × 365 = 930.75 kWh.
Money saved = 930.75 × $0.12 = **$111.69**.
*Teaching note:* stopping at one bulb gives $11.17, which is the intended trap.

**12.** **1 bulb is lit.** In parallel each bulb has its own branch. Bulb 1's branch is broken (unscrewed) and bulb 2's branch is broken (dead filament), but bulb 3's branch is untouched and complete.

**13.** **$21.38 saved per month.**
Share moving from on-peak to off-peak = 70% − 40% = 30%.
Energy shifted = 750 × 0.30 = 225 kWh.
Price difference = $0.182 − $0.087 = $0.095 per kWh.
Saving = 225 × $0.095 = **$21.38**.
*Teaching note:* you never need the other 70% of the bill, only the part that moved.

**14.** **W is in series with a parallel group of X, Y and Z.**

```
              ┌──(X)──┐
   ──(W)──────┼──(Y)──┼──────
              └──(Z)──┘
```

X and Y each go out **alone**, so they must sit on their own separate branches, meaning they are in parallel with each other. W's removal darkens everything, so W must sit on the single **shared** path that feeds the whole parallel group.
If all four were in series, removing X would darken everything, which contradicts the evidence. If all four were in parallel, removing W would darken only W, which also contradicts it.

**15.** **400 000 homes.**
Station output = 500 MW × 1 000 = 500 000 kW.
Homes = 500 000 ÷ 1.25 = **400 000**, roughly the size of Windsor plus Kingston.
