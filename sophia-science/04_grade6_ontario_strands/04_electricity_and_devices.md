## 📖 Part 1: Real-World Context & Concept Spotlight

At Niagara Falls, the Sir Adam Beck generating stations take water that has fallen 90 m and turn it into electricity for millions of Ontario homes.
Gravity pulls the water down, the water spins a turbine, the turbine spins a generator, and the generator pushes electrons through wires.
Every step is an **energy transformation**.
Nothing is created; energy only changes form.

### 💡 Concept 1: Static Against Current

| | Static electricity | Current electricity |
|---|---|---|
| What the charge does | Builds up and **stays put** | **Keeps flowing** |
| Needs a circuit? | No | Yes, a complete one |
| Ends by | A sudden **discharge** (a spark) | Switching off |
| Ontario example | Boots on a dry January carpet, then a doorknob | A lamp switched on |

"Static" means stationary. Rubbing transfers electrons onto you; touching metal lets them jump across at once.

### 💡 Concept 2: The Four Parts of a Complete Circuit

```
        ┌────────── conductor (wire) ──────────┐
        │                                      │
     ┌──┴──┐                               ╭───┴───╮
     │  ⊣⊢ │ SOURCE                        │  (☀)  │  LOAD
     │ 3 V │ (battery)                     │ bulb  │
     └──┬──┘                               ╰───┬───╯
        │                                      │
        └──────── switch ──╱ ──────────────────┘
                        (opens the loop)
```

1. **Source** pushes the charge: a battery or a generating station.
2. **Conductors** carry it: the wires.
3. **Load** transforms it into something useful: the bulb.
4. **An unbroken path**. Break the loop anywhere and everything stops.

### 💡 Concept 3: Series Against Parallel

```
   SERIES: one single loop                PARALLEL: a branch per bulb

   ┌──(☀)──(☀)──(☀)──┐                  ┌───┬────┬────┬───┐
   │                  │                  │  (☀)  (☀)  (☀) │
   └────── battery ───┘                  └───┴────┴────┴───┘
                                              battery

   Voltage is SHARED between bulbs        Each bulb gets the FULL voltage
   More bulbs = all DIMMER                More bulbs = all the SAME brightness
   One bulb out = ALL go dark             One bulb out = the others STAY LIT
   Used in: old light strings, fuses      Used in: house wiring, car lights
```

**The test that tells them apart:** unscrew one bulb.
If everything dies, it is series. If the rest stay lit, it is parallel.

### 💡 Concept 4: Conductors and Insulators

| Conductors (current passes) | Insulators (current blocked) |
|---|---|
| Copper wire, aluminium foil | Rubber, plastic, glass |
| Canadian toonie (metals) | Dry wood, dry paper |
| Pencil graphite (carbon) | Ceramic |
| **Salt water** and tap water | Pure distilled water |

**Why:** conductors have charged particles that are **free to move**.
Metals have loosely held electrons. Salt water has dissolved charged particles.
This is exactly why electricity and water together are dangerous.

### 💡 Concept 5: Energy Transformations in Devices

| Device | Electrical energy becomes |
|---|---|
| LED bulb | Light |
| Electric kettle | Thermal (heat) |
| Speaker | Sound |
| Fan or blender | Kinetic (motion) |
| Phone charging | Chemical (stored in the battery) |
| Old incandescent bulb | Light **and a lot of waste heat** |

### 💡 Concept 6: Paying for Energy in Ontario

$$\text{kWh} = \frac{\text{watts}}{1000} \times \text{hours}$$

Ontario electricity costs roughly **$0.12 per kWh**.

| Device | Power | Hours/day | kWh/day | Cost/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LED lights | 60 W | 6 | 0.36 | $0.04 |
| Refrigerator | 150 W | 24 | 3.60 | $0.43 |
| Electric kettle | 1 500 W | 0.25 | 0.38 | $0.05 |
| Clothes dryer | 3 000 W | 1 | 3.00 | $0.36 |

**Ontario's generation mix:** about half **nuclear**, roughly a quarter **hydroelectricity**, and the rest wind, solar, biofuel and natural gas.
Hydro, wind and solar are **renewable**. Natural gas is **non-renewable** and releases carbon dioxide.
Gas plants are switched on to cover **peak** demand, so shifting heavy appliance use off peak genuinely lowers emissions.
