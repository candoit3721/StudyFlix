## ⚡ Round 3: Periodic Table Trivia Showdown (Multiple Choice)

**1. What does the "Atomic Number" of an element tell you?**  
A) How heavy the element is in pounds  
B) The number of protons inside the atom's center  
C) How many dollars it costs to buy  
D) What year the element was discovered  

**2. Which two elements combine to make pure water ($H_2O$)?**  
A) Helium and Oxygen  
B) Hydrogen and Oxygen  
C) Carbon and Nitrogen  
D) Sodium and Chlorine  

**3. Why are Helium, Neon, and Argon called "Noble Gases"?**  
A) They wear golden crowns  
B) They are the most dangerous gases  
C) They are super stable and almost never react or bond with other elements  
D) They smell like perfume  

**4. What element gives red blood cells their color and helps carry oxygen to your muscles?**  
A) Gold ($Au$)  
B) Iron ($Fe$)  
C) Aluminum ($Al$)  
D) Neon ($Ne$)  

**5. Which element is used in toothpaste to fight off sugar bugs and protect tooth enamel?**  
A) Fluorine ($F$)  
B) Sulfur ($S$)  
C) Copper ($Cu$)  
D) Hydrogen ($H$)
