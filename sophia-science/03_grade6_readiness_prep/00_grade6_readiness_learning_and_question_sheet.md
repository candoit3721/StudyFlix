## 🎢 Unit 2: Forces, Motion & Roller Coaster Physics

### 📖 The Science Story: The Physics of the "Mega Drop" Roller Coaster
When you ride a massive roller coaster:
1. The motorized chain pulls your car up to the peak of the tallest hill. As you climb higher against Earth's **Gravity**, your car stores up massive **Gravitational Potential Energy (Stored Energy)**!
2. At the very top, speed is zero, but **Potential Energy is MAXIMUM**!
3. As you crest the hill and plunge downward, gravity accelerates you faster and faster — **Potential Energy converts into Kinetic Energy (Motion Energy)**!
4. At the lowest point of the drop, your speed is highest and **Kinetic Energy is MAXIMUM**!

```
                  🎢 PE = 100% (High up, zero speed)
                      /\
                     /  \
      ⚡ PE turns   /    \
         into KE!  /      \
                  /        \
                 /          \  🏎️ KE = 100% (Fastest speed at bottom)
                /____________\
```

---

### 🏎️ Newton's 3 Laws of Motion (The Rules of the Universe):
* **1st Law (Inertia)**: Things keep doing what they're doing (resting or moving) unless pushed! *(Why you lurch forward when a car brakes!)*
* **2nd Law ($F = m \times a$)**: Heavier mass requires more force to push or accelerate!
* **3rd Law (Action & Reaction)**: Every action has an equal and opposite reaction! *(Rockets push fire down to fly up!)*

## 🕵️‍♀️ Unit 3: The Scientific Method & Variables Masterclass

### 📖 The Science Story: Designing a Fair Experiment Like a Pro
In Middle School, you won't just memorize science facts — you will design real investigations! The secret formula is the **I-D-C Variable Rule**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE 3 SCIENTIFIC VARIABLES                      │
│                                                                        │
│  1. 🎛️ INDEPENDENT VARIABLE (IV): What "I" change on purpose!          │
│     (The one variable you are testing).                                │
│                                                                        │
│  2. 📏 DEPENDENT VARIABLE (DV): What you MEASURE (the data/result).    │
│     (Changes in response to the IV).                                   │
│                                                                        │
│  3. 🔒 CONTROLLED VARIABLES (Constants): Kept IDENTICAL!               │
│     (Ensures a fair test where only the IV causes the result).         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
