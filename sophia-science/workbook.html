Unit 5: Biodiversity & Classification (Strand B)

Biodiversity is the variety of life at three levels: within a species, between species, and of ecosystems. The Six Kingdoms: Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista, Eubacteria, Archaebacteria - the last two are the ONLY ones with no nucleus. The 10% Rule: only about a tenth of the energy at one level passes to the next, which is why top predators are rare.

Unit 6: Flight & the Four Forces (Strand D)

Four forces, two opposing pairs: LIFT opposes WEIGHT; THRUST opposes DRAG. Balanced means equal and opposite, NOT zero. Bernoulli's Principle: where air moves faster, its pressure is lower, so the curved upper wing surface makes low pressure and the higher pressure below pushes up. Properties of air: it has mass, takes up space, can be compressed, and exerts pressure.

Unit 7: Space & the Solar System (Strand E)

Rotation (spinning on its axis, 24 h) causes day and night. Revolution (orbiting the Sun, 365.25 d) makes one year. Seasons come from the 23.5° axial TILT, not distance - Earth is actually closest to the Sun in early January. Mass (kg) never changes; weight is a force in newtons, W = m × g. Canada built the Canadarm2 on the International Space Station.

Unit 8: Electricity & Devices (Strand C)

Static electricity builds up and stays put, ending in a discharge. Current electricity keeps flowing around a complete circuit. SERIES = one loop, voltage is shared, one bulb out means ALL go dark. PARALLEL = a branch per bulb, each gets full voltage, one bulb out and the rest STAY LIT (this is how houses are wired). Energy cost: kWh = watts ÷ 1000 × hours, at about $0.12/kWh in Ontario.
