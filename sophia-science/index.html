Baking Soda + Vinegar Reaction

When solid baking soda mixes with acidic vinegar, bonds break and reform instantly to create water, sodium acetate, and fizzy Carbon Dioxide gas (CO₂)!

🔍 4 Clues / Evidence of Change:
  • 🫧 Gas Production: Rapid foaming bubbles fill the flask and inflate the balloon!
  • 🌡️ Temperature Drop: Endothermic reaction absorbs heat (feels icy cold!).
  • ⚖️ Conservation of Mass: Total mass before mixing (110g) = Total mass after mixing (110g)!
💡 Try This at Home: Put 2 spoons of baking soda inside a deflated balloon, fit it over a water bottle with vinegar, and lift the balloon to watch it self-inflate!

1. What on Earth is an Atom?

Protons, Neutrons, Electrons, and Dmitri Mendeleev's Secret Table

▼

Imagine breaking a chocolate chip cookie into smaller and smaller crumbs. The smallest piece that still acts like that substance is an Atom!

🔬 Interactive Graphic Simulator Available

Inside Every Atom:

  • Protons (p+): Positive charge in the center. The number of protons is the Atomic Number — it gives the element its identity!
  • Neutrons (n0): Neutral particles packed inside the nucleus with protons.
  • Electrons (e-): Fast negatively charged particles zooming around the outside in orbits.

2. Matter & Kitchen Chemistry

Physical vs. Chemical Changes, Solutions, and Conservation of Mass

▼

Every Saturday morning breakfast is a science lab! Let's understand the two major types of changes:

🧪 Visual Reaction Simulator Available

Physical Change

Only changes size, shape, or state (solid/liquid/gas). NO new substance is created!

Examples: Melting ice, dissolving sugar, tearing paper.

Chemical Change

Bonds break and rearrange to create a BRAND NEW substance!

4 Clues: Gas bubbles 🫧, Color change 🎨, Temp change 🔥, New odor/smell 🍞.

The Magic Scale Rule (Conservation of Mass):

Matter cannot be created out of nowhere, and it cannot be destroyed! If you mix 10g sugar with 100g water in a cup, the total weight is exactly 110g!

3. Ecosystems & The Sun's Energy Flow

Photosynthesis, Food Webs, and the 10% Energy Pyramid Rule

▼

Every jump, run, and thought you make is powered by energy that originally came from the Sun!

☀️ Sun ➔ 🌱 Producers (Plants) ➔ 🐰 Herbivores (Consumers) ➔ 🦊 Carnivores ➔ 🍄 Decomposers

The 10% Energy Rule:

When an animal eats another organism, only ~10% of the energy is stored. The other 90% is used for running, breathing, and body heat!

5. Roller Coaster Physics & Newton's Laws (Grade 6 Prep)

Potential Energy vs. Kinetic Energy, Gravity, Friction & Newton's 3 Laws

▼
🎢 Kinetic Simulator Available

The Roller Coaster Secret:

  • Potential Energy (PE): Stored energy. Maximum at the highest hill!
  • Kinetic Energy (KE): Motion energy. Maximum at the fastest, lowest bottom!

Newton's 3 Laws in Plain English:

  1. 1st Law (Inertia): Objects keep resting or moving unless pushed (wear seatbelts!).
  2. 2nd Law (F = ma): Heavier objects need stronger pushes to accelerate.
  3. 3rd Law (Action/Reaction): For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction (skateboards & rockets!).

6. The Scientific Method & Variables (Grade 6 Prep)

The #1 Middle School Skill: Independent, Dependent, and Controlled Variables

▼

The I-D-C Variable Formula:

  • 🎛️ Independent Variable (IV): The ONE thing "I" change on purpose to test.
  • 📏 Dependent Variable (DV): What you MEASURE (the data/result).
  • 🔒 Controlled Variables (Constants): Everything kept strictly SAME for a fair test!
