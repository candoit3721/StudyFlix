# 🏛️ Sophia's Ancient Rome Quest & STEM Studio ⚔️
*Enriched Social Studies, Architectural Engineering, Latin & Military Cryptography for Ontario Grade 5 & 6*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Sophia's Ancient Rome Quest** is an interactive, multi-disciplinary learning studio built for StudyFlix. It explores the history, democratic foundations, civil engineering marvels, everyday culture, and mathematical cryptography of ancient Rome.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. 🗺️ **Empire Adventure Map**: 6 structured learning quests tracking Sophia's progress across the Republic, Engineering, Caesar's Era, and Daily Life.
2. 🏛️ **Interactive Engineering & STEM Lab**:
   * **Keystone Arch Demonstrator**: Dynamic SVG showing lateral compression forces and load distribution.
   * **Aqueduct Gravity Flow Simulator**: Slider adjusting gradient ($0.2\% - 0.5\%$) to deliver fresh mountain water to Rome.
   * **Colosseum & Hypoegum Explorer**: Interactive cutaways of 28 pulley lifts, vomitoria exits, and velarium sunshade.
   * **Pantheon Concrete Chemistry**: The chemistry of volcanic ash (*pozzolana*) and unreinforced concrete dome tapering.
   * **Layered Roman Highway Cross-Section**: 4-layer strata of the *Via Appia*.
3. 📖 **Story & Inquiry Learning Hub**: 6 illustrated narrative lessons connecting Roman innovations with modern Canadian legal systems, French/English Latin roots, and chemistry symbols ($Au, Fe, Pb$).
4. 🎯 **Interactive Roman Quiz Arena**: 4 quiz game modes with 30+ curated questions, hints, rich pedagogical explanations, and XP rewards.
5. 🔢 **Roman Numerals & Caesar Cipher Lab**:
   * Dual-directional Roman numeral converter ($1 \leftrightarrow I$, $2026 \leftrightarrow MMXXVI$).
   * Interactive Caesar Cipher decryption tool with 6 historical military missions sent by Julius Caesar.
6. 🏆 **Imperial Trophy Room**: 8 unlockable badges with sound effects and confetti celebrations.
7. 🖨️ **Master Printable Workbook**: [`00_SOPHIA_COMPLETE_ANCIENT_ROME_WORKBOOK.md`](./00_SOPHIA_COMPLETE_ANCIENT_ROME_WORKBOOK.md) with Tier 1, Tier 2, and Waterloo CEMC contest-level Tier 3 challenges + complete answer key.
