Chapter 1

🕰️ Anatomy of the Analog Clock & Hand Mechanics

An analog clock is a circle with 12 big hours and 60 small minute ticks.

🔴 The Hour Hand Secret: The short red hand does NOT stay frozen! At 3:30, it points halfway between 3 and 4. At 3:55, it is almost at 4, but it is STILL in the 3-o'clock hour!
🔵 The Minute Hand Secret: Skip-count by 5s! Multiply the number the long hand points to by 5. (e.g. Points to 7 $\rightarrow 7 \times 5 = 35\text{ minutes}$).
Chapter 2

☀️ AM vs. PM & Canadian 24-Hour Airport Time

There are 24 hours in a full Earth day. We split it into two 12-hour halves:

  • 🌅 A.M. (Morning): From 12:00 Midnight to 11:59 AM (Breakfast at 7:30 AM, Morning Recess at 10:15 AM).
  • 🌇 P.M. (Afternoon/Night): From 12:00 Noon to 11:59 PM (Lunch at 12:15 PM, Bedtime at 8:30 PM).
  • ✈️ 24-Hour Time (Pearson Airport & VIA Rail): Add 12 for afternoon hours ($3:40\text{ PM} = 15:40$, $8:15\text{ PM} = 20:15$).
Chapter 3

🏔️ Elapsed Time: The "Mountains, Hills & Rocks" Strategy

How much time passed between Start Time and End Time?

🏔️ Mountain = +1 Hour ⛰️ Hill = +15 or +10 Min 🪨 Rock = +1 Min

Example: From 2:45 PM to 5:10 PM $\rightarrow$ 2:45 + 1hr (3:45) + 1hr (4:45) + 15m (5:00) + 10m (5:10) = 2 Hours and 25 Minutes!
