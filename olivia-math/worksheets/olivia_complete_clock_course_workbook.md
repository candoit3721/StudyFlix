# Unit 1: Anatomy of the Analog Clock & Precision Time-Telling 🕰️

## 📖 1.1 The Science of the Clock Face

An analog clock is a circle divided into **12 big hour sections** and **60 small minute ticks** ($360^\circ$ in total circle geometry).

```
                            12 (00 min / O'clock)
                       11          1
                   10                  2
                  9 ─── (Center Axis) ─── 3 (15 min / Quarter Past)
                    8                  4
                        7          5
                             6 (30 min / Half Past)
```

### 🔴 The Short Red Hand (The Hour Hand):
* Takes **12 hours** to make one full circle.
* **Crucial Rule**: The hour hand does NOT stay frozen on a number! It creeps slowly between numbers as the minutes tick by.
  * At $3:00$, it points directly at $3$.
  * At $3:30$, it points **halfway between $3$ and $4$**.
  * At $3:55$, it points **almost directly at $4$**, but it is STILL in the 3-o'clock hour until the minute hand hits the 12!

### 🔵 The Long Blue Hand (The Minute Hand):
* Takes **60 minutes (1 hour)** to make one full circle.
* Each number on the clock represents **5 minutes** of time ($1 = 5\text{ min}, 2 = 10\text{ min}, \dots, 11 = 55\text{ min}, 12 = 60\text{ min} / 00$).
* **Memory Trick**: Multiply the number the long hand points to by $5$! ($7 \times 5 = 35\text{ minutes}$).

# Unit 2: AM vs. PM, Noon, Midnight & 24-Hour Time ☀️🌙

## 📖 2.1 The 24-Hour Earth Day Cycle

There are **24 hours in a full day**, but a standard analog clock only shows 12 hours. That's why we divide the day into two 12-hour halves:

* 🌅 **A.M. (*Ante Meridiem* = Before Midday)**: From **12:00 Midnight** to **11:59 Morning**.
  * Waking up ($7:00\text{ AM}$), eating breakfast ($7:30\text{ AM}$), morning recess at school ($10:15\text{ AM}$).
* 🌇 **P.M. (*Post Meridiem* = After Midday)**: From **12:00 Noon** to **11:59 Night**.
  * Eating lunch ($12:15\text{ PM}$), school dismissal ($3:30\text{ PM}$), dinner ($6:00\text{ PM}$), bedtime ($8:30\text{ PM}$).

```
[ 12:00 Midnight ] ─── (A.M. Morning) ───> [ 12:00 Noon ] ─── (P.M. Afternoon/Night) ───> [ 12:00 Midnight ]
```

### ✈️ 2.2 Canadian 24-Hour Time (Airport & Train Schedules):
In Canadian airports (like Toronto Pearson $YYZ$ or Ottawa $YOW$) and on VIA Rail trains, schedules use **24-Hour Time** to avoid AM/PM confusion:
* For hours past noon, **add 12**:
  * $1:00\text{ PM} = 13:00$
  * $4:30\text{ PM} = 16:30$ ($4 + 12 = 16$)
  * $8:15\text{ PM} = 20:15$ ($8 + 12 = 20$)

# Unit 3: Elapsed Time: The "Mountains, Hills & Rocks" Strategy ⏱️

## 📖 3.1 The Number Line Jump Strategy

**Elapsed Time** is the amount of time that passes from a **Start Time** to an **End Time**.

```
Formula:  Start Time  +  Elapsed Time  =  End Time
          End Time    -  Start Time    =  Elapsed Time
```

To solve any elapsed time problem without getting confused when crossing the 60-minute hour mark, Canadian math champions use the **"Mountains, Hills & Rocks" Jump Strategy**:

```
      🏔️ MOUNTAIN = 1 Hour (60 minutes)
      ⛰️ HILL     = 10 or 15 Minutes
      🪨 ROCK     = 1 Minute
```

### 🌟 Example Problem:
*Olivia starts skating on the Rideau Canal at $2:45\text{ PM}$ and finishes at $5:10\text{ PM}$. How much time did she skate?*

```
2:45 PM  ──( 🏔️ +1 hr )──>  3:45 PM  ──( 🏔️ +1 hr )──>  4:45 PM
4:45 PM  ──( ⛰️ +15 min )──>  5:00 PM (Friendly Hour Mark!)
5:00 PM  ──( ⛰️ +10 min )──>  5:10 PM

Total Elapsed Time = 1 hr + 1 hr + 15 min + 10 min = 2 Hours and 25 Minutes!
```

# COMPLETE ANSWER KEY & TEACHER/PARENT SOLUTIONS 🔑

### Unit 1 Solutions:
* **Q1.** **40 minutes** ($8 \times 5 = 40$).
* **Q2.** **C) Most of the way toward 7, but still in the 6-hour zone** (at 45 min, it is $\frac{3}{4}$ of the way to 7).
* **Q3.** Quarter **past** 4 | Half **past** 7 | Quarter **to** 10.
* **Q4.** **10:22** (Hour between 10 and 11; Minute = $4 \times 5 = 20 + 2\text{ ticks} = 22$).

### Unit 2 Solutions:
* **Q5.** Oatmeal: **AM** | Afternoon recess: **PM** | Night stars: **PM** | Lunch noon: **PM** (12:00 PM is noon).
* **Q6.** $3:40\text{ PM} = \mathbf{15:40}$ ($3 + 12 = 15$) | $19:15 = \mathbf{7:15\text{ PM}}$ ($19 - 12 = 7$).

### Unit 3 Solutions:
* **Q7.1.** $8:15\text{ AM}$ to $11:30\text{ AM}$: $8:15 \xrightarrow{+3\text{ hrs}} 11:15 \xrightarrow{+15\text{ min}} 11:30 = \mathbf{3\text{ hours and } 15\text{ minutes}}$.
* **Q7.2.** $1:50\text{ PM}$ to $4:15\text{ PM}$: $1:50 \xrightarrow{+10\text{ min}} 2:00 \xrightarrow{+2\text{ hrs}} 4:00 \xrightarrow{+15\text{ min}} 4:15 = \mathbf{2\text{ hours and } 25\text{ minutes}}$.
* **Q7.3.** $10:35\text{ AM}$ to $1:20\text{ PM}$: $10:35 \xrightarrow{+2\text{ hrs}} 12:35 \xrightarrow{+25\text{ min}} 1:00 \xrightarrow{+20\text{ min}} 1:20 = \mathbf{2\text{ hours and } 45\text{ minutes}}$.
* **Q8.** $4:10\text{ PM} - 10\text{ min} = 4:00\text{ PM} - 35\text{ min} = \mathbf{3:25\text{ PM}}$.

### Unit 4 Solutions:
* **Problem 1 (Blue Jays Outing)**:
  * Part A: $12:45\text{ PM} - 35\text{ min} = \mathbf{12:10\text{ PM}}$.
  * Part B: $1:05\text{ PM} + 2\text{ hr } 40\text{ min} = 1:05 + 2\text{ hr} = 3:05 + 40\text{ min} = \mathbf{3:45\text{ PM}}$.
  * Part C: $3:45\text{ PM} + 20\text{ min} = \mathbf{4:05\text{ PM}}$.
* **Problem 2 (Algonquin Hike)**:
  * Total duration $= 1\text{ hr } 35\text{ min} + 25\text{ min} + 1\text{ hr } 15\text{ min} = 2\text{ hr } 75\text{ min} = 3\text{ hours and } 15\text{ minutes}$.
  * $9:15\text{ AM} + 3\text{ hr } 15\text{ min} = \mathbf{12:30\text{ PM}}$.
* **Problem 3 (The Baker)**:
  * Timer A: $2:20 + 25\text{ min} = \mathbf{2:45\text{ PM}}$.
  * Timer B: $2:20 + 40\text{ min} = \mathbf{3:00\text{ PM}}$.
  * Timer C: $2:20 + 1\text{ hr } 10\text{ min} = \mathbf{3:30\text{ PM}}$.

### Unit 5 Solutions (Contest Puzzles):
* **Contest Puzzle 1 (Mirror Clock)**:
  * **Answer: 8:20**.
  * *Explanation*: The vertical axis is 12 and 6. In a mirror reflection, the 3 reflects to the 9, and the 8 reflects to the 4. The minute hand at the 8 (40 min) reflects to the 4 (20 min). The hour hand near 4 reflects to just past 8. Real time is $8:20$!
* **Contest Puzzle 2 (Faulty Clock)**:
  * From 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM is 5 hours.
  * $5\text{ hours} \times 4\text{ minutes lost/hour} = \mathbf{20\text{ minutes lost}}$.
  * Displayed time $= 1:00\text{ PM} - 20\text{ minutes} = \mathbf{12:40\text{ PM}}$.
* **Contest Puzzle 3 (Cross-Canada Flight)**:
  * Step 1: $10:00\text{ AM EST} + 5\text{ hr } 15\text{ min} = 3:15\text{ PM EST}$.
  * Step 2: $3:15\text{ PM} - 3\text{ hours} = \mathbf{12:15\text{ PM PST}}$ (Local Vancouver time).
* **Contest Puzzle 4 (Overlapping Hands)**:
  * **Answer: B) Approximately 1:05 and a half PM (around 1:05:27 PM)**.
  * *Explanation*: At 1:00, the minute hand is at 12 and the hour hand is at 1. In 5 minutes (at 1:05), the minute hand reaches the 1, but during those 5 minutes, the hour hand has crept $\frac{5}{60} = \frac{1}{12}$ of the way toward the 2! So they overlap slightly past 1:05 ($1\text{ hour } 5\frac{5}{11}\text{ minutes}$).
