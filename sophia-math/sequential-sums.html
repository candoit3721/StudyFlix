📋 Formula & Step-by-Step Breakdown

ONTARIO STANDARD
Master Formula
Sum = (N × (N + 1)) / 2
🧮 Live Calculation Steps
Why the formula works: Pairing 1 with N, 2 with (N-1), etc. gives pairs that all equal (N + 1). Since there are N / 2 pairs, the sum is exactly N(N+1)/2.
Common Trap: Don't forget to divide by 2! 10 × 11 = 110, but the true sum is half: 55.

🌟 1. The Young Gauss Pairing Trick (1787)

When asked to add numbers from 1 to 100, Gauss noticed that the numbers could be folded in half:

1 + 100 = 101
2 + 99 = 101
3 + 98 = 101
...
50 + 51 = 101
────────────────────────
50 pairs × 101 = 5,050!

🚀 2. The Universal Formula: A to B

To add numbers from any starting point $A$ to ending point $B$ (e.g. $5$ to $25$ or $5$ to $10{,}000$):

1. Number of Terms: N = B - A + 1
2. Pair Sum: First + Last = A + B
3. Total Sum: (N × (A + B)) / 2
────────────────────────
5 to 25: N = 21, Pair = 30
Sum = (21 × 30) / 2 = 315!

🛡️ 3. The Prefix Subtraction Method

Calculate the full sum to $B$, then subtract the missing prefix from $1$ to $(A - 1)$:

Sum(5 to 25) = Sum(1..25) - Sum(1..4)
Sum(1..25) = (25 × 26)/2 = 325
Sum(1..4) = (4 × 5)/2 = 10
────────────────────────
325 - 10 = 315 ✓ (Dual Proof!)

⚖️ 4. Consecutive Evens & Odds

Special geometric patterns for even and odd integers:

Evens: 2 + 4 + ... + 2k = k(k + 1)
• First 10 evens (2..20) = 10 × 11 = 110

Odds: 1 + 3 + ... + (2k - 1) = k²
• First 10 odds (1..19) = 10² = 100!
