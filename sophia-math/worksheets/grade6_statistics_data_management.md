## 📖 Concept Spotlight: The Big 4 of Statistics

| Metric | Definition & Purpose | Key Memory Hack |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mean** | The mathematical average of all values in a dataset. | **"Mean is Mean"** — Add all numbers up, then divide by the total count: $\text{Mean} = \frac{\sum x}{N}$. |
| **Median** | The exact middle value when data is sorted in order. | **"Median is the Middle"** — Put numbers in order. If count is odd, pick the middle number. If count is even, take the average of the two middle numbers. |
| **Mode** | The value(s) that occur most frequently. | **"Mode = Most Often"** — Count frequencies. Can be unimodal (1 mode), bimodal (2 modes), or have no mode (all frequencies equal). |
| **Range** | The spread or difference between the highest and lowest values. | **"Range = High Minus Low"** — $\text{Range} = \text{Maximum} - \text{Minimum}$. |

## 🔑 Comprehensive Answer Key & Teacher/Parent Solutions

1. **$16\text{ cm}$** — $\text{Sum} = 14 + 18 + 12 + 16 + 20 = 80$. $\text{Mean} = 80 \div 5 = 16\text{ cm}$.
2. **$4\text{ goals}$** — $\text{Sum} = 3 + 5 + 2 + 6 + 4 + 4 = 24$. $\text{Mean} = 24 \div 6 = 4\text{ goals}$.
3. **$19^\circ\text{C}$** — $\text{Sum} = 16 + 19 + 22 + 15 + 23 = 95$. $\text{Mean} = 95 \div 5 = 19^\circ\text{C}$.
4. **$94$** — Total points needed for 4 tests $= 4 \times 90 = 360$. Current sum $= 86 + 92 + 88 = 266$. Required 4th score $= 360 - 266 = 94$.
5. (a) **$[ 28, 31, 42, 48, 55 ]$**  
   (b) **$42$** — The 3rd (middle) value is $42$.
6. **$24.5\text{ seconds}$** — Ordered: $[ 21.4, 22.0, 23.2, \mathbf{24.5}, 25.1, 26.8, 27.0 ]$. Middle element is $24.5$.
7. (a) **$[ 14, 18, 19, 22, 25, 30 ]$**  
   (b) **$20.5$** — Two middle numbers are $19$ and $22$. $\text{Median} = \frac{19 + 22}{2} = \frac{41}{2} = 20.5$.
8. **$80.5$** — Ordered: $[ 68, 75, 79, \mathbf{82}, \mathbf{85}, 88, 90, 94 ]$. Two middle numbers are $82$ and $85$. $\text{Median} = \frac{82 + 85}{2} = \frac{167}{2} = 80.5$.
9. **$6$** — Size $6$ appears 4 times (highest frequency).
10. **$12\text{ and }15$** (Bimodal) — Both $12$ and $15$ appear twice.
11. **no mode** — Every number appears exactly once.
12. **$18$** — Before adding $x$, $14$ appears twice and $18$ appears twice (tied). To make $18$ the unique mode, $x$ must be $18$ so it appears 3 times.
13. **$16^\circ\text{C}$** — $\text{Max} = 25^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{Min} = 9^\circ\text{C}$. $\text{Range} = 25 - 9 = 16^\circ\text{C}$.
14. **$20^\circ\text{C}$** — $\text{Range} = 4 - (-16) = 4 + 16 = 20^\circ\text{C}$.
15. **$73$** — $\text{Max} = \text{Min} + \text{Range} = 45 + 28 = 73$.
16. **$58$** — $\text{Min} = \text{Max} - \text{Range} = 92 - 34 = 58$.
17. **$23$** — Let the 5 integers in ascending order be $a \le b \le c \le d \le e$.  
    - Sum $= 5 \times 12 = 60$.  
    - Median $c = 10$.  
    - Since unique mode is $8$ and $8 < 10$, we must have $a = b = 8$.  
    - So the set is $8, 8, 10, d, e$.  
    - $d + e = 60 - (8 + 8 + 10) = 60 - 26 = 34$.  
    - To maximize $e$, we must minimize $d$. Since $10 < d \le e$ and $d \ne 8$, the smallest integer $d$ can be is $11$.  
    - If $d = 11$, then $e = 34 - 11 = 23$. (Notice all elements $8, 8, 10, 11, 23$ have unique mode 8). Greatest integer is $23$.
18. (a) **$21.5$** — $\text{Sum} = 172 \div 8 = 21.5$.  
    (b) **$21$** — Sorted: $[ 15, 17, 18, \mathbf{19}, \mathbf{23}, 23, 26, 31 ]$. $\text{Median} = \frac{19 + 23}{2} = 21$.  
    (c) **$23$** — Appears twice.  
    (d) **$16$** — $\text{Max} - \text{Min} = 31 - 15 = 16$.
19. **$25$** — Sum of original 5 numbers $= 5 \times 20 = 100$. New sum $= 100 + 50 = 150$. New mean $= 150 \div 6 = 25$.
20. **$26$** — Original sum of 7 numbers $= 7 \times 14 = 98$. New sum of 6 numbers $= 6 \times 12 = 72$. The removed number $= 98 - 72 = 26$.
