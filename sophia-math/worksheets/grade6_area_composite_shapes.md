## 🎯 Reference Guide: Formulas & Strategies

| Shape | Formula | Key Strategy / Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Triangle** | $A = \frac{1}{2} b h$ | $h$ is strictly perpendicular ($90^\circ$) to base $b$. |
| **Parallelogram** | $A = b \times h$ | Base $\times$ vertical height (ignore slant side for area). |
| **Trapezoid** | $A = \frac{a + b}{2} \times h$ | Average parallel bases and multiply by perpendicular height. |
| **Rhombus / Kite** | $A = \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{2}$ | Multiply perpendicular diagonals and divide by 2. |
| **Circle** | $A = \pi r^2$ | Use $\pi \approx 3.14$; $r = \frac{\text{diameter}}{2}$. |
| **Circular Ring** | $A = \pi(R^2 - r^2)$ | Outer circle area minus inner hole area. |
| **Composite Additive** | $A = A_1 + A_2 + \dots$ | Split into non-overlapping standard rectangles/triangles. |
| **Composite Subtractive** | $A = A_{\text{box}} - A_{\text{holes}}$ | Outer bounding box minus unshaded cutout spaces. |

## 🔑 Complete Answer Key & Solutions

1. **Parallelogram**: $A = 18 \times 12 = \mathbf{216\text{ cm}^2}$.
2. **Triangle**: $A = \frac{1}{2} \times 22 \times 15 = 11 \times 15 = \mathbf{165\text{ cm}^2}$.
3. **Trapezoid**: $A = \frac{14 + 26}{2} \times 8.5 = 20 \times 8.5 = \mathbf{170\text{ m}^2}$.
4. **Rhombus Sign**: $A = \frac{60 \times 60}{2} = \mathbf{1\,800\text{ cm}^2}$.
5. **Splash Pad**: Radius $r = 4\text{ m} \implies A = 3.14 \times 4^2 = 3.14 \times 16 = \mathbf{50.24\text{ m}^2}$.
6. **Missing Triangle Height**: $108 = \frac{1}{2} \times 18 \times h = 9h \implies h = \frac{108}{9} = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}}$.
7. **Missing Trapezoid Base**: $135 = \frac{12 + b_2}{2} \times 9 \implies \frac{135}{9} = 15 \implies \frac{12 + b_2}{2} = 15 \implies 12 + b_2 = 30 \implies b_2 = \mathbf{18\text{ cm}}$.
8. **Missing Kite Diagonal**: $144 = \frac{16 \times d_2}{2} = 8 \times d_2 \implies d_2 = \frac{144}{8} = \mathbf{18\text{ cm}}$.
9. **L-Shaped Playground**: Total bounding $= 20 \times 16 = 320\text{ m}^2$. Shed $= 8 \times 9 = 72\text{ m}^2$. Area $= 320 - 72 = \mathbf{248\text{ m}^2}$.
10. **House Profile**: Base $= 6 \times 4 = 24\text{ m}^2$. Roof $= \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 2.5 = 7.5\text{ m}^2$. Total $= 24 + 7.5 = \mathbf{31.5\text{ m}^2}$.
11. **Pool Deck**: Outer dimensions $= (10 + 3) \times (5 + 3) = 13\text{ m} \times 8\text{ m} = 104\text{ m}^2$. Pool $= 10 \times 5 = 50\text{ m}^2$. Walkway $= 104 - 50 = \mathbf{54\text{ m}^2}$.
12. **Running Track Annulus**: $A = 3.14 \times (24^2 - 20^2) = 3.14 \times (576 - 400) = 3.14 \times 176 = \mathbf{552.64\text{ m}^2}$.
13. **Cross-Shaped Garden**:
    - (a) Area $= 5 \times (3 \times 3) = 5 \times 9 = \mathbf{45\text{ m}^2}$.
    - (b) Perimeter $= 12 \text{ exposed edges} \times 3\text{ m} = \mathbf{36\text{ m}}$.
14. **Trapezoid with Cutout**: Trapezoid $= \frac{10 + 16}{2} \times 6 = 13 \times 6 = 78\text{ m}^2$. Window $= 2 \times 3 = 6\text{ m}^2$. Net $= 78 - 6 = \mathbf{72\text{ m}^2}$.
15. **Octagon from Square**: Square $= 16 \times 16 = 256\text{ cm}^2$. 4 corners $= 4 \times (\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 4) = 4 \times 8 = 32\text{ cm}^2$. Octagon $= 256 - 32 = \mathbf{224\text{ cm}^2}$.
16. **Semicircle Crescent**: Large semicircle $= \frac{1}{2} \times 3.14 \times 10^2 = 157\text{ cm}^2$. Small semicircle $= \frac{1}{2} \times 3.14 \times 6^2 = 56.52\text{ cm}^2$. Shaded $= 157 - 56.52 = \mathbf{100.48\text{ cm}^2}$.
17. **Quadrilateral $AECF$**: Square $= 10 \times 10 = 100\text{ cm}^2$. Unshaded $\triangle ABE = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 3 = 15\text{ cm}^2$. Unshaded $\triangle ADF = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 4 = 20\text{ cm}^2$. Area $(AECF) = 100 - (15 + 20) = \mathbf{65\text{ cm}^2}$.
18. **Two Overlapping Squares**: When a square's vertex is at the centre of an identical square, the lines from the centre to the square's edges divide the square into 4 rotationally symmetric regions. Area is always exactly $\frac{1}{4}$ of the square: $\frac{8 \times 8}{4} = \mathbf{16\text{ cm}^2}$.
19. **Rhombus Height**:
    - (a) Area $= \frac{24 \times 10}{2} = \mathbf{120\text{ cm}^2}$.
    - (b) Rhombus is also a parallelogram ($A = \text{base} \times \text{height}$): $120 = 13 \times h \implies h = \frac{120}{13} \approx \mathbf{9.23\text{ cm}}$ (or $\mathbf{\frac{120}{13}\text{ cm}}$).
20. **T-Shaped Tiling Cost**:
    - Total area $= (12 \times 3) + (4 \times 8) = 36 + 32 = 68\text{ m}^2$.
    - Base cost $= 68 \times \$32 = \$2\,176.00$.
    - HST ($13\%$) $= \$2\,176 \times 0.13 = \$282.88$.
    - Total cost with HST $= \$2\,176 + \$282.88 = \mathbf{\$2\,458.88}$.
