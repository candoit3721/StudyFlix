## 💡 The "Fraction of a Remainder" Strategy Guide

In multi-step fraction problems, the word **"of"** means **multiply ($\times$)**, but you must carefully distinguish between:
1. **Fraction of the ORIGINAL TOTAL**: e.g., $\frac{1}{4}$ of the starting $4\frac{4}{5}\text{ L}$.
2. **Fraction of the REMAINDER**: e.g., $\frac{1}{9}$ of **what she still had**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE 2-WAY SOLVING FRAMEWORK (BAR MODEL & MATH)           │
│                                                                        │
│  [Total: 4 4/5 L = 24/5 L]                                             │
│  ├─ GAVE TO BROTHER: 1/4 of Total = 1/4 × 24/5 = 6/5 L                 │
│  └─ REMAINDER 1: (3/4 of Total) = 18/5 L                               │
│      ├─ DRANK: 1/9 of Remainder 1 = 1/9 × 18/5 = 2/5 L                 │
│      └─ REMAINDER 2: (8/9 of Remainder 1) = 16/5 L                     │
│          ├─ USED FOR PARTY: 1/2 of Remainder 2 = 1/2 × 16/5 = 8/5 L    │
│          └─ LEFT OVER: 1/2 of Remainder 2 = 8/5 L                      │
│                                                                        │
│  ★ FINAL COMPARISON: (Left Over) − (Brother) = 8/5 L − 6/5 L = 2/5 L   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 📖 Guided Warmup (The Problem in the Photo)

**Problem 0:** Kamala had $4\frac{4}{5}\text{ L}$ of juice. After giving $\frac{1}{4}$ of it to her brother, she drank $\frac{1}{9}$ of what she still had. She then used half of the remaining juice for a party. What is the difference between what Kamala had left and what she gave to her brother?

- **Step 1 (Convert to improper fraction):** $4\frac{4}{5} = \frac{4 \times 5 + 4}{5} = \frac{24}{5}\text{ L}$.
- **Step 2 (Brother's share):** $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{24}{5} = \frac{6}{5} = 1\frac{1}{5}\text{ L}$.
- **Step 3 (Remainder 1):** $\frac{24}{5} - \frac{6}{5} = \frac{18}{5}\text{ L}$ (or $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{24}{5} = \frac{18}{5}\text{ L}$).
- **Step 4 (Drank amount):** $\frac{1}{9} \times \frac{18}{5} = \frac{2}{5}\text{ L}$.
- **Step 5 (Remainder 2):** $\frac{18}{5} - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{16}{5}\text{ L}$ (or $\frac{8}{9} \times \frac{18}{5} = \frac{16}{5}\text{ L}$).
- **Step 6 (Party use & Leftover):** Used $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{16}{5} = \frac{8}{5}\text{ L}$, so Kamala had $\frac{8}{5} = 1\frac{3}{5}\text{ L}$ left.
- **Step 7 (Final Difference):** $\text{Left Over} - \text{Brother} = \frac{8}{5} - \frac{6}{5} = \frac{2}{5}\text{ L}$ (or $400\text{ mL}$).

## 🔑 Comprehensive Step-by-Step Solutions & Answer Key

### Problem 1: Maple Lemonade Stand
- Total $= 5\frac{1}{4} = \frac{21}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Morning sale: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{21}{4} = \frac{7}{4} = 1\frac{3}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Remainder 1: $\frac{21}{4} - \frac{7}{4} = \frac{14}{4} = \frac{7}{2}\text{ L}$.
- Afternoon sale: $\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{7}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1\text{ L} = \frac{4}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Remainder 2: $\frac{7}{2} - 1 = \frac{5}{2}\text{ L}$.
- Helpers: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{5}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Left over: $\frac{5}{2} - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{5}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}\text{ L}$.
- **Answers:**  
  **(a)** $\mathbf{1\frac{1}{4}\text{ L}}$ (or $\frac{5}{4}\text{ L} = 1.25\text{ L}$).  
  **(b)** Morning minus Leftover $= \frac{7}{4} - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}\text{ L}}$ (or $0.5\text{ L}$).

### Problem 2: Silk Ribbon Craft Project
- Total $= 6\frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Birthday gift: $\frac{1}{5} \times \frac{20}{3} = \frac{4}{3} = 1\frac{1}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Remainder 1: $\frac{20}{3} - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{16}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Hair bows: $\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{16}{3} = \frac{48}{24} = 2\text{ m} = \frac{6}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Remainder 2: $\frac{16}{3} - \frac{6}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Science board: $1\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Final remaining: $\frac{10}{3} - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{6}{3} = 2\text{ m}$.
- **Answers:**  
  **(a)** $\mathbf{2\text{ m}}$ used for hair bows.  
  **(b)** $\mathbf{2\text{ m}}$ remaining on the spool.

### Problem 3: Organic Wildflower Honey
- Total $= 7\frac{1}{2} = \frac{15}{2}\text{ kg}$.
- Bakery: $\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{15}{2} = \frac{30}{10} = 3\text{ kg}$.
- Remainder 1: $\frac{15}{2} - 3 = \frac{15}{2} - \frac{6}{2} = \frac{9}{2}\text{ kg}$.
- Farmers' market jars: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{9}{2} = \frac{3}{2} = 1.5\text{ kg}$.
- Remainder 2: $\frac{9}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3\text{ kg}$.
- Candles: $\frac{1}{2} \times 3 = \frac{3}{2} = 1.5\text{ kg}$.
- Final left in container: $3 - 1.5 = 1.5\text{ kg} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ kg}$.
- **Answers:**  
  **(a)** $\mathbf{3\text{ kg}}$ sent to bakery.  
  **(b)** Difference: $3\text{ kg} - 1.5\text{ kg} = \mathbf{1.5\text{ kg}}$ (or $1\frac{1}{2}\text{ kg}$).

### Problem 5: Working Backwards with Blueberry Muffins
- **Working Backwards Step by Step:**
  - Before picnic basket (giving half): $9 \times 2 = 18$ muffins (this was Remainder 2).
  - Remainder 2 was $(1 - \frac{1}{3}) = \frac{2}{3}$ of Remainder 1.  
    Therefore, Remainder $1 = 18 \div \frac{2}{3} = 18 \times \frac{3}{2} = 27$ muffins.
  - Remainder 1 was $(1 - \frac{1}{4}) = \frac{3}{4}$ of the Initial Total.  
    Therefore, Initial Total $= 27 \div \frac{3}{4} = 27 \times \frac{4}{3} = 36$ muffins.
- **Verification:**  
  $36 \xrightarrow{\text{gave } 1/4 \ (9)} 27 \xrightarrow{\text{gave } 1/3 \ (9)} 18 \xrightarrow{\text{gave half } (9)} 9$ left. Correct!
- **Answer:** $\mathbf{36\text{ muffins}}$.

### Problem 6: Science Lab Solution Dilution
- Total $= 3\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Trial 1: $\frac{1}{5} \times \frac{15}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\text{ L}$.
- Remainder 1: $\frac{15}{4} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{12}{4} = 3\text{ L}$.
- Trial 2: $\frac{1}{6} \times 3 = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\text{ L}$.
- Remainder 2: $3 - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}\text{ L}$.
- Trial 3: $\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}\text{ L}$.
- Final left in beaker: $\frac{5}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1\text{ L}$.
- **Answers:**  
  **(a)** $\mathbf{1\frac{1}{2}\text{ L}}$ (or $\frac{3}{2}\text{ L} = 1.5\text{ L}$) used in Trial 3.

### Problem 7: High-Enquiry Multi-Step Story (Apples & Pies)
- Total $= 12\frac{4}{5} = \frac{64}{5}\text{ kg}$.
- Chef: $\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{64}{5} = \frac{3 \times 8}{5} = \frac{24}{5} = 4\frac{4}{5}\text{ kg}$ ($4.8\text{ kg}$).
- Remainder 1: $\frac{64}{5} - \frac{24}{5} = \frac{40}{5} = 8\text{ kg}$.
- School snack bags: $\frac{1}{4} \times 8 = 2\text{ kg}$.
- Remainder 2: $8 - 2 = 6\text{ kg}$.
- Community kitchen donation: $\frac{2}{3} \times 6 = 4\text{ kg} = \frac{20}{5}\text{ kg}$.
- Difference between chef ($\frac{24}{5}\text{ kg}$) and kitchen donation ($4\text{ kg} = \frac{20}{5}\text{ kg}$):  
  $\frac{24}{5} - \frac{20}{5} = \frac{4}{5}\text{ kg}$ ($0.8\text{ kg} = 800\text{ g}$).
- **Answer:** $\mathbf{\frac{4}{5}\text{ kg}}$ (or $0.8\text{ kg}$).

### Problem 8: The Ultimate Contest Challenge (Gauss Olympiad Tier 3)
- **Work Backwards:**
  - After Ethan took $\frac{1}{2}$ of Remainder 2 plus $4$, $16$ marbles remained.  
    $\frac{1}{2}$ of Remainder $2 = 16 + 4 = 20 \implies \text{Remainder } 2 = 20 \times 2 = 40$ marbles.
  - Sophia took $\frac{1}{5}$ of Remainder 1, leaving $\frac{4}{5}$ of Remainder 1.  
    $\frac{4}{5} \times \text{Remainder } 1 = 40 \implies \text{Remainder } 1 = 40 \times \frac{5}{4} = 50$ marbles.
  - Lucas took $\frac{2}{7}$ of the initial total, leaving $\frac{5}{7}$ of the initial total.  
    $\frac{5}{7} \times \text{Total} = 50 \implies \text{Total} = 50 \times \frac{7}{5} = 70$ marbles.
- **Verification:**  
  - Total $= 70$. Lucas takes $\frac{2}{7} \times 70 = 20 \implies 50$ left.  
  - Sophia takes $\frac{1}{5} \times 50 = 10 \implies 40$ left.  
  - Ethan takes $\frac{1}{2} \times 40 + 4 = 20 + 4 = 24 \implies 40 - 24 = 16$ left. Exactly matches!
- **Answer:** $\mathbf{70\text{ marbles}}$.
