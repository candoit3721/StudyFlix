## 💡 Quick Reminders

1. **Change mixed numbers to improper fractions first.** $4\frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{5}$
2. **"of" means multiply.** $\frac{1}{4}$ of $\frac{24}{5}$ $= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{24}{5}$
3. **Watch the WHOLE.** Is the fraction of the *starting amount* or of *what is left*?
4. **Shortcut:** If you give away $\frac{1}{4}$, then $\frac{3}{4}$ is left. Multiply by $\frac{3}{4}$ to jump straight to the remainder.
5. **Draw a bar model** for each step — cut the bar, then cut the leftover piece again.

## 🔑 Answer Key with Step-by-Step Solutions

### Problem 1: Orange Juice
- Total $= 6\frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3}\text{ L}$
- Neighbour: $\frac{1}{5} \times \frac{20}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\text{ L}$
- Still had: $\frac{20}{3} - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{16}{3}\text{ L}$
- Drank: $\frac{1}{8} \times \frac{16}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{16}{3} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{14}{3}\text{ L}$
- Smoothies (half): $\frac{7}{3}\text{ L}$ → Left: $\frac{7}{3}\text{ L}$
- Difference: $\frac{7}{3} - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{3}{3}$
- **Answer: $\mathbf{1\text{ L}}$**

---

### Problem 2: Baking Flour
- Total $= 5\frac{5}{6} = \frac{35}{6}\text{ kg}$
- Bread: $\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{35}{6} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}\text{ kg}$ → Remaining: $\frac{25}{6}\text{ kg}$
- Cookies: $\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{25}{6} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}\text{ kg}$ → Remaining: $\frac{15}{6} = \frac{5}{2}\text{ kg}$
- Mom: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{5}{6}\text{ kg}$ → Kept: $\frac{5}{2} - \frac{5}{6} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}\text{ kg}$
- **(a)** $\frac{5}{3} + \frac{5}{3} = \frac{10}{3} = \mathbf{3\frac{1}{3}\text{ kg}}$
- **(b)** $\frac{10}{6} - \frac{5}{6} = \mathbf{\frac{5}{6}\text{ kg}}$

---

### Problem 3: Ribbon Crafts
- Total $= 8\frac{1}{4} = \frac{33}{4}\text{ m}$
- Gift: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{33}{4} = \frac{11}{4}\text{ m}$ → Remaining: $\frac{22}{4} = \frac{11}{2}\text{ m}$
- Bows: $\frac{3}{11} \times \frac{11}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ m}$ → Remaining: $\frac{11}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = 4\text{ m}$
- Banner: $\frac{3}{4} \times 4 = 3\text{ m}$ → Left: $1\text{ m}$
- Difference: $\frac{11}{4} - 1 = \frac{7}{4}$
- **Answer: $\mathbf{1\frac{3}{4}\text{ m}}$**

---

### Problem 4: Painting the Fence
- Total $= 3\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{4}\text{ L}$
- Fence: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{15}{4} = \frac{5}{4}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{10}{4} = \frac{5}{2}\text{ L}$
- Spilled: $\frac{1}{10} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{1}{4}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{10}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\text{ L}$
- Shed: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} = \frac{6}{4}\text{ L}$ → Left: $\frac{3}{4}\text{ L}$
- **(a)** $\mathbf{\frac{1}{4}\text{ L}}$ (250 mL)
- **(b)** $\frac{5}{4} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}\text{ L}}$

---

### Problem 5: Milk for Breakfast
- Total $= 7\frac{1}{5} = \frac{36}{5}\text{ L}$
- Neighbour: $\frac{1}{6} \times \frac{36}{5} = \frac{6}{5}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{30}{5} = 6\text{ L}$
- Pancakes: $\frac{1}{4} \times 6 = \frac{3}{2}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{9}{2}\text{ L}$
- Drank: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{9}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ L}$ → Left: $3\text{ L}$
- **(a)** $\mathbf{3\text{ L}}$
- **(b)** $3 - \frac{6}{5} = \frac{15}{5} - \frac{6}{5} = \frac{9}{5} = \mathbf{1\frac{4}{5}\text{ L}}$

---

### Problem 6: Allowance
- Book: $\frac{2}{5} \times 90 = \$36$ → Remaining: $\$54$
- Snacks: $\frac{1}{6} \times 54 = \$9$ → Remaining: $\$45$
- Savings: $\frac{2}{3} \times 45 = \$30$ → Wallet: $\$15$
- Difference: $36 - 15$
- **Answer: $\mathbf{\$21}$**

---

### Problem 7: Sugar Three Times
- Total $= 4\frac{1}{2} = \frac{9}{2}\text{ kg}$
- Day 1: used $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{9}{2} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ kg}$ → Left: $3\text{ kg}$
- Day 2: used $\frac{1}{3} \times 3 = 1\text{ kg}$ → Left: $2\text{ kg}$
- Day 3: used $\frac{1}{3} \times 2 = \frac{2}{3}\text{ kg}$ → Left: $\frac{4}{3}\text{ kg}$
- Shortcut: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{27}$ of the original is left.
- **(a)** $\mathbf{1\frac{1}{3}\text{ kg}}$
- **(b)** $\mathbf{\frac{8}{27}}$  (check: $\frac{8}{27} \times \frac{9}{2} = \frac{4}{3}$ ✓)
- **(c)** $\frac{3}{2} - \frac{4}{3} = \frac{9}{6} - \frac{8}{6} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{6}\text{ kg}}$

---

### Problem 8: Watering the Garden
- Total $= 9\frac{3}{8} = \frac{75}{8}\text{ L}$
- Flowers: $\frac{1}{5} \times \frac{75}{8} = \frac{15}{8}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{60}{8} = \frac{15}{2}\text{ L}$
- Dog bowl: $\frac{15}{2} - \frac{3}{2} = 6\text{ L}$ remaining
- Bike: $\frac{5}{6} \times 6 = 5\text{ L}$ → Left: $1\text{ L}$
- Difference: $\frac{15}{8} - 1 = \frac{7}{8}$
- **Answer: $\mathbf{\frac{7}{8}\text{ L}}$**

---

### Problem 9: Sharing Lemonade
- Total $= 6\frac{3}{4} = \frac{27}{4}\text{ L}$
- Lucas got: $\frac{2}{9} \times \frac{27}{4} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}\text{ L}$ → Sophia still had: $\frac{21}{4}\text{ L}$
- Sophia drank: $\frac{1}{7} \times \frac{21}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\text{ L}$ → Remaining: $\frac{18}{4} = \frac{9}{2}\text{ L}$
- Party: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{2} = 3\text{ L}$ → **Sophia left: $\frac{3}{2}\text{ L}$**
- Lucas drank: $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{1}{2}\text{ L}$ → **Lucas left: $1\text{ L}$**
- **Answer: Sophia has $\mathbf{\frac{1}{2}\text{ L}}$ more.**

---

### Problem 10: Working Backwards
- Before the party she had double what was left: $\frac{5}{4} \times 2 = \frac{5}{2}\text{ L}$
- After drinking $\frac{1}{4}$, $\frac{3}{4}$ remained: $\frac{5}{2} \div \frac{3}{4} = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\text{ L}$
- After giving $\frac{1}{3}$, $\frac{2}{3}$ remained: $\frac{10}{3} \div \frac{2}{3} = \frac{10}{3} \times \frac{3}{2} = 5\text{ L}$
- Check: $5 \to$ gave $\frac{5}{3} \to \frac{10}{3} \to$ drank $\frac{5}{6} \to \frac{5}{2} \to$ half $\to \frac{5}{4}$ ✓
- **(a)** $\mathbf{5\text{ L}}$
- **(b)** Brother got $\frac{5}{3}\text{ L}$. $\frac{5}{3} - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{20}{12} - \frac{15}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{5}{12}\text{ L}}$ (brother got more)
