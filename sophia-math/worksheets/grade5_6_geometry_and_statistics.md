## 🔑 Answer Key & Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

1. **$99\text{ cm}^2$** — $A = \frac{1}{2} \times 18 \times 11 = 9 \times 11 = 99$.
2. **$66\text{ in}^2$** — $A = \frac{8 + 14}{2} \times 6 = 11 \times 6 = 66$.
3. (a) **$480\text{ cm}^3$** — $V = 12 \times 8 \times 5 = 480$.  
   (b) **$392\text{ cm}^2$** — $SA = 2(12 \times 8 + 12 \times 5 + 8 \times 5) = 2(96 + 60 + 40) = 2(196) = 392$.
4. **$180\text{ cm}^3$** — Base Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 4 = 12\text{ cm}^2$; Volume $= 12 \times 15 = 180$.
5. **$56^\circ$** — $90^\circ - 34^\circ = 56^\circ$.
6. **$62^\circ$** — $180^\circ - 118^\circ = 62^\circ$.
7. **$48^\circ$** — $90^\circ - 42^\circ = 48^\circ$.
8. (a) **$12.56\text{ cm}$** — $C = 3.14 \times 4 = 12.56$.  
   (b) **$12.56\text{ cm}^2$** — Radius $r = 2$; $A = 3.14 \times 2^2 = 3.14 \times 4 = 12.56$.
9. **$20$** — Sum $= 14 + 18 + 15 + 22 + 18 + 25 + 28 = 140$; Mean $= 140 \div 7 = 20$.
10. **$18$** — Sorted: $[ 14, 15, 18, \mathbf{18}, 22, 25, 28 ] \rightarrow$ middle is $18$.
11. **$18$** — Appears twice.
12. **$14$** — $28 - 14 = 14$.
13. **$[ 68, 68, 70, 72, 72, 74, 75, 81 ]$**
14. **$72$** — Average of 4th and 5th items: $\frac{72 + 72}{2} = 72$.
15. **$68\text{ and }72$** (Bimodal — each appears twice).
16. **$13^\circ\text{F}$** — $81 - 68 = 13$.
17. **$\frac{1}{2}$** — Even numbers: $\{2, 4, 6\} \rightarrow \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$.
18. **$\frac{2}{5}$** — Total $= 4 + 6 + 5 = 15$; Blue $= \frac{6}{15} = \frac{2}{5}$ (or $40\%$).
19. **$\frac{11}{15}$** — Not red $= 6 + 5 = 11 \rightarrow \frac{11}{15}$.
20. **$\frac{3}{8}$** — Numbers $> 5$: $\{6, 7, 8\} \rightarrow \frac{3}{8}$.
