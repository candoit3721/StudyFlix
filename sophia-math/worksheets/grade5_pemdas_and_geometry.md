## 🔑 Answer Key & Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

1. **$48$** — Parentheses first: $(9 - 4) = 5$; then $6 \times 5 = 30$; then $18 + 30 = 48$.
2. **$25$** — $(24 - 8) = 16$; $16 \div 4 = 4$; $7 \times 3 = 21$; $4 + 21 = 25$.
3. **$15$** — $5^2 = 25$; inside parentheses: $(4 + 6) = 10$; $25 - 10 = 15$.
4. **$24$** — Innermost: $(15 - 7) = 8$; $8 \div 2 = 4$; brackets: $4 + 4 = 8$; $3 \times 8 = 24$.
5. **$27$** — $2^3 = 8$; $8 \times 3 = 24$; $40 - 24 + 11 = 16 + 11 = 27$.
6. **$4$** — First parentheses: $8 + 12 = 20$; second parentheses: $10 - 5 = 5$; $20 \div 5 = 4$.
7. **$20$** — Left-to-right mult/div: $72 \div 8 = 9$; $9 \times 2 = 18$; $18 + 5 - 3 = 20$.
8. **$10$** — $6^2 = 36$; $36 - 16 = 20$; $2 \times 20 = 40$; $40 \div 4 = 10$.
9. **$23$** — First part: $50 - 30 = 20$; second part: $27 \div 9 = 3$; $20 + 3 = 23$.
10. **$75$** — Innermost: $(8 + 2) = 10$; $3 \times 10 = 30$; $30 - 5 = 25$; $100 - 25 = 75$.
11. **$112\text{ cm}^2$** — $A = 14 \times 8 = 112$.
12. **$51.4\text{ m}$** — $P = 2(16.5 + 9.2) = 2(25.7) = 51.4$.
13. **$42\text{ cm}^2$** — $A = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 7 = 6 \times 7 = 42$.
14. **$270\text{ in}^3$** — $V = 9 \times 5 \times 6 = 270$.
15. **$64\text{ m}^3$** — $V = 4^3 = 64$.
16. **$240\text{ ft}^3$** — $V = 10 \times 6 \times 4 = 240$.
17. **$60^\circ$** — $180^\circ - (55^\circ + 65^\circ) = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$.
18. **$94\text{ cm}^2$** — $SA = 2(5 \times 4 + 5 \times 3 + 4 \times 3) = 2(20 + 15 + 12) = 2(47) = 94$.
19. **$144\text{ ft}^2$** — Side length $= 48 \div 4 = 12\text{ ft}$; Area $= 12 \times 12 = 144\text{ sq ft}$.
20. **$120\text{ cm}^2$** — $A = b \times h = 15 \times 8 = 120$.
