### Challenge Word Problems

Show all formulas, equations, and calculation steps clearly.

1. **The School Bake Sale (Fraction Operations & Money)**  
   Sophia is baking chocolate chip cookies for the school charity fair. Each batch requires $2\frac{1}{4}$ cups of flour and $\frac{3}{4}$ cup of butter.
   - (a) How much flour will Sophia need to make 6 batches?  
   - (b) If Sophia sells each cookie for \$1.25 and bakes 72 cookies in total, how much money will she raise if all cookies sell out?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

2. **The Bookstore Shopping Trip (Decimals & Percentages)**  
   Sophia goes shopping with a \$50 gift card. She chooses a science encyclopedia for \$24.00 and two graphic novels for \$9.50 each.
   - (a) The bookstore has a special promotion giving a $15\%$ discount off the entire purchase. What is the total discount amount?  
   - (b) After the $15\%$ discount is applied, how much money remains on Sophia's gift card?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

3. **Paint Mixing Ratios (Ratios & Proportions)**  
   An art teacher is mixing teal paint by combining blue paint and green paint in a ratio of $5 : 3$.
   - (a) If the teacher uses 45 fluid ounces of blue paint, how many fluid ounces of green paint are needed?  
   - (b) What is the total volume of teal paint made?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

4. **The Road Trip (Speed, Distance & Unit Rates)**  
   A family drives on the highway for 4.5 hours at a steady average speed of 62 miles per hour.
   - (a) How many miles did they travel in total?  
   - (b) If their car gets 28 miles per gallon of gasoline, approximately how many gallons of gas did they use (round to the nearest tenth)?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

5. **Bedroom Carpeting & Baseboards (Geometry: Area & Perimeter)**  
   Sophia's rectangular bedroom is 14 feet long and 11 feet wide.
   - (a) How many square feet of new carpet are needed to cover the entire floor?  
   - (b) If the carpet costs \$4.50 per square foot, what is the total cost of the carpet?  
   - (c) Sophia wants to put a wooden decorative border around the perimeter of the room. The door opening is 3 feet wide (which does not need a border). How many feet of wooden border are needed?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

6. **Aquarium Volume & Water Filling (Geometry & Rates)**  
   A rectangular glass aquarium is 30 inches long, 16 inches wide, and 18 inches tall.
   - (a) What is the maximum volume of the aquarium in cubic inches?  
   - (b) The aquarium is filled with water up to a height of 15 inches (leaving 3 inches of space at the top). How many cubic inches of water are currently in the tank?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

7. **The Library Mystery (Algebraic Word Problem)**  
   Sophia has 3 times as many mystery books as science fiction books. After she donates 6 mystery books to the library and buys 2 new science fiction books, she has 44 total books remaining. How many mystery books and how many science fiction books did Sophia originally have?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

8. **Test Score Improvement (Percentages & Averages)**  
   On her first 4 math quizzes, Sophia scored 84, 88, 92, and 96.
   - (a) What is Sophia's current mean (average) quiz score?  
   - (b) What score must Sophia earn on her 5th quiz to raise her overall average score to 91?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

9. **Grocery Unit Price Comparison (Decimals & Proportions)**  
   Store Alpha sells an 18-ounce jar of organic honey for \$7.20. Store Beta sells a 28-ounce jar of the same honey for \$9.80.
   - (a) What is the price per ounce at Store Alpha?  
   - (b) What is the price per ounce at Store Beta?  
   - (c) Which store offers the better buy, and how much do you save per ounce?  
   *Space for work:*  
   <br><br><br><br>

10. **The Consecutive Integer Riddle (Pre-Algebra)**  
    The sum of three consecutive integers is equal to 84. What are the three integers?  
    *Space for work:*  
    <br><br><br><br>

## 🔑 Answer Key & Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

1. **School Bake Sale:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{13\frac{1}{2}\text{ cups}}$ — $2\frac{1}{4} \times 6 = \frac{9}{4} \times 6 = \frac{54}{4} = 13\frac{1}{2}$ cups.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{\$90.00}$ — $72 \times \$1.25 = \$90.00$.

2. **Bookstore Shopping Trip:**  
   - Subtotal $= \$24.00 + 2 \times \$9.50 = \$24.00 + \$19.00 = \$43.00$.  
   - (a) $\mathbf{\$6.45}$ discount — $0.15 \times \$43.00 = \$6.45$.  
   - Total paid $= \$43.00 - \$6.45 = \$36.55$.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{\$13.45}$ remaining — $\$50.00 - \$36.55 = \$13.45$.

3. **Paint Mixing Ratios:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{27\text{ fl oz}}$ — Scale factor $= 45 \div 5 = 9$; Green $= 3 \times 9 = 27$ fl oz.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{72\text{ fl oz}}$ — Total $= 45 + 27 = 72$ fl oz.

4. **Road Trip:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{279\text{ miles}}$ — Distance $= 62 \times 4.5 = 279$ miles.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{10.0\text{ gallons}}$ — $279 \div 28 \approx 9.96 \approx 10.0$ gallons.

5. **Bedroom Carpeting & Baseboards:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{154\text{ sq ft}}$ — Area $= 14 \times 11 = 154$ sq ft.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{\$693.00}$ — Cost $= 154 \times \$4.50 = \$693.00$.  
   - (c) $\mathbf{47\text{ feet}}$ — Perimeter $= 2(14 + 11) = 50$ ft; Border $= 50 - 3 = 47$ ft.

6. **Aquarium Volume:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{8,640\text{ in}^3}$ — Total Volume $= 30 \times 16 \times 18 = 8,640\text{ cu in}$.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{7,200\text{ in}^3}$ — Water Volume $= 30 \times 16 \times 15 = 7,200\text{ cu in}$.

7. **Library Mystery:**  
   - Let $s =$ science fiction books, then mystery books $= 3s$.  
   - After change: $(3s - 6) + (s + 2) = 44 \rightarrow 4s - 4 = 44 \rightarrow 4s = 48 \rightarrow s = 12$.  
   - Originally: $\mathbf{12\text{ science fiction books}}$ and $\mathbf{36\text{ mystery books}}$ ($3 \times 12$).

8. **Test Score Improvement:**  
   - (a) $\mathbf{90}$ — Sum $= 84 + 88 + 92 + 96 = 360$; Average $= 360 \div 4 = 90$.  
   - (b) $\mathbf{95}$ — Needed total for 5 quizzes $= 91 \times 5 = 455$; 5th Quiz $= 455 - 360 = 95$.

9. **Grocery Unit Price Comparison:**  
   - (a) Store Alpha: $\mathbf{\$0.40\text{ per oz}}$ — $\$7.20 \div 18 = \$0.40$/oz.  
   - (b) Store Beta: $\mathbf{\$0.35\text{ per oz}}$ — $\$9.80 \div 28 = \$0.35$/oz.  
   - (c) $\mathbf{\text{Store Beta is cheaper by }\$0.05\text{ per oz}}$.

10. **Consecutive Integer Riddle:**  
    - Let integers be $n, n+1, n+2$.  
    - $n + (n+1) + (n+2) = 84 \rightarrow 3n + 3 = 84 \rightarrow 3n = 81 \rightarrow n = 27$.  
    - The three integers are $\mathbf{27, 28, \text{ and } 29}$.
