## 🔑 Answer Key & Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

1. **$2 : 3$** — $\text{GCF}(24, 36) = 12$; $24 \div 12 = 2$, $36 \div 12 = 3$.
2. **$x = 49$** — Cross-multiply: $4x = 7 \times 28 = 196 \rightarrow x = 196 \div 4 = 49$ (or scale factor $\times 7$).
3. **$n = 25$** — Scale factor: $40 \div 8 = 5$; $n = 5 \times 5 = 25$.
4. **$25\text{ mph}$** — $75 \div 3 = 25$.
5. **\$3.00** — Unit price $= \$4.80 \div 8 = \$0.60$ per apple; $5 \times \$0.60 = \$3.00$.
6. **$18\text{ girls}$** — Total parts $= 2 + 3 = 5$ parts; 1 part $= 30 \div 5 = 6$ students; Girls $= 3 \times 6 = 18$.
7. **$12$** — $0.15 \times 80 = 12$.
8. **$100$** — $0.40 \times 250 = 100$.
9. **$75\%$** — $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4} = 0.75 = 75\%$.
10. **\$90.00** — Discount $= 0.25 \times \$120 = \$30$; Sale price $= \$120 - \$30 = \$90$.
11. **\$8.10** — Tip $= 0.18 \times \$45 = \$8.10$.
12. **\$85.60** — Tax $= 0.07 \times \$80 = \$5.60$; Total $= \$80 + \$5.60 = \$85.60$.
13. **$13$** — Opposite signs: $28 - 15 = 13$ (positive since $28 > 15$).
14. **$-32$** — Same signs: $-(18 + 14) = -32$.
15. **$31$** — Subtracting a negative is addition: $12 + 19 = 31$.
16. **$-42$** — Same signs: $-(25 + 17) = -42$.
17. **$-56$** — Negative $\times$ Positive $=$ Negative: $-56$.
18. **$54$** — Negative $\times$ Negative $=$ Positive: $+54$.
19. **$9$** — Negative $\div$ Negative $=$ Positive: $+9$.
20. **$27$** — $|-34| = 34$, $|12| = 12$, $|-5| = 5 \rightarrow 34 - 12 + 5 = 27$.
