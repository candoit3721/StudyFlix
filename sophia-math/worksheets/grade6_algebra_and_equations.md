## 🔑 Answer Key & Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

1. **$x = 28$** — Subtract 17 from both sides: $x = 45 - 17 = 28$.
2. **$y = 82$** — Add 28 to both sides: $y = 54 + 28 = 82$.
3. **$m = 13$** — Divide both sides by 6: $m = 78 \div 6 = 13$.
4. **$a = 91$** — Multiply both sides by 7: $a = 13 \times 7 = 91$.
5. **$x = 5.7$** — Subtract 3.4 from both sides: $x = 9.1 - 3.4 = 5.7$.
6. **$w = 6$** — Divide by 4.5: $w = 27 \div 4.5 = 6$.
7. **$x = 7$** — Step 1: $3x = 29 - 8 = 21$; Step 2: $x = 21 \div 3 = 7$.
8. **$y = 10$** — Step 1: $5y = 36 + 14 = 50$; Step 2: $y = 50 \div 5 = 10$.
9. **$n = 32$** — Step 1: $\frac{n}{4} = 15 - 7 = 8$; Step 2: $n = 8 \times 4 = 32$.
10. **$p = 8$** — Step 1: $7p = 47 + 9 = 56$; Step 2: $p = 56 \div 7 = 8$.
11. **$k = 48$** — Step 1: $\frac{k}{6} = 5 + 3 = 8$; Step 2: $k = 8 \times 6 = 48$.
12. **$x = 9$** — Divide by 2: $x + 5 = 14 \rightarrow x = 14 - 5 = 9$ (or expand $2x + 10 = 28 \rightarrow 2x = 18 \rightarrow x = 9$).
13. **$9x$** — $(8 + 5 - 4)x = 9x$.
14. **$10a + 2b$** — $(7a + 3a) + (4b - 2b) = 10a + 2b$.
15. **$12y + 20$** — $4 \times 3y + 4 \times 5 = 12y + 20$.
16. **$39$** — $4(6) + 3(5) = 24 + 15 = 39$.
17. **$24$** — $5^2 - 2(4) + 7 = 25 - 8 + 7 = 24$.
18. **$6$** — $(3(4) + 6) / 3 = (12 + 6) / 3 = 18 / 3 = 6$.
19. **$3n - 7 = 26$**
20. **$n = 11$** — $3n = 26 + 7 = 33 \rightarrow n = 33 \div 3 = 11$.
